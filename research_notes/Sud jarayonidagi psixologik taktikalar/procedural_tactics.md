## Key Question 1: Catalog of procedural tactics (name UZ/RU, what it is, rule UZ EPC + RU APK, example, stage, signals, counter, student mistakes, abuse line and sanctions)

### Takeaway
The Uzbek EPC 2018 gives the AI opponent a full lawful toolkit. The main tools are: powers checks (arts. 61-64), recusal (20-24), the evidence rules (66-76, 80-82), disclosure and adverse inference (68 parts 3-5), third parties (47-48), counterclaim (160-161), adjournment (171, at most 3 times and 10 days each), break (172, up to 3 days) and pretrial-claim objections (107 item 5, 118 part 4). Substantive defences come from the Civil Code: limitation (CC art. 153, on a party's application only, before the decision) and penalty reduction (CC art. 326). Unlike the Russian APK (art. 41 part 2, art. 111 part 2, art. 159 part 5), the Uzbek EPC has **no general "abuse of procedural rights" sanction article**. It has a general duty of good faith (art. 42 part 2), targeted fines (arts. 69, 125-126), cost-shifting for pretrial-claim breaches (118 part 4), hard caps on adjournments (171) and a bar on repeat recusal (22 part 4). Students should use these targeted levers rather than cite an "abuse" article that does not exist in Uzbek law.

### Cited Findings

#### 0. Background rules the AI opponent exploits (all tactics)
- **Good-faith duty (UZ)**: EPC art. 42 part 2 says participants have the procedural duties set by the Code "and must exercise all their procedural rights in good faith" ("барча процессуал ҳуқуқлардан инсофли равишда фойдаланиши керак"). Part 1 lists their rights: to file recusals, present evidence, file motions (илтимосномалар), object to other parties' motions, and so on. No sanction clause is attached. [UZ-VERIFIED] — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **Adversarial principle and equality**: EPC art. 9 (тарафларнинг тортишуви ва тенг ҳуқуқлилиги) and art. 43 part 5 (parties have equal procedural rights). [UZ-VERIFIED] — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU equivalent**: APK art. 41 part 2: abuse of procedural rights brings the adverse consequences provided by the APK. Art. 111 part 2: the court may put all costs on the abuser if the abuse caused a hearing to fail or the case to drag. Art. 159 part 5: the court may refuse a motion (application) if it was not filed in time through the applicant's fault and is aimed at delay. Art. 9 part 2: a party bears the risk of not performing procedural actions. [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]. Practitioners confirm that art. 159 part 5 and art. 111 are the standard anti-delay tools — [Pravo.ru, «Злоупотребления процессуальными правами: как отбиться от них в суде»](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/); [Consultant podborka «Затягивание судебного процесса АПК»](https://www.consultant.ru/law/podborki/zatyagivanie_sudebnogo_processa_apk/)
- **UZ fines**: EPC art. 125: up to 50 BHM (base calculation units) for citizens and up to 200 BHM for legal entities, "in cases provided by this Code". Fines on officials are paid from their personal funds. Art. 126: the procedure. A fine on a person present is decided in the same hearing, a ruling (ажрим) is issued and can be appealed, and the appeal does not suspend it. The Code provisions that refer to fines include art. 58 (specialist), art. 69 (failure to produce requested evidence) and art. 98 (failure to enforce an interim-measure ruling). [UZ-VERIFIED] — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **UZ courtroom order**: EPC art. 167. Participants address the court as «Ҳурматли суд!» and stand when speaking. The presiding judge warns a person who disturbs order; a repeat offender may be removed from the courtroom. The article provides no fine for disorder. [UZ-VERIFIED] — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU fines**: APK arts. 119-120. Up to 5,000 RUB for citizens, 30,000 RUB for officials and 100,000 RUB for organisations; disrespect to the court can be fined under art. 119 together with art. 154 part 5. [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]. RAPSI's summary of the 2021 VS RF Plenum on APK first instance says interrupting participants, lengthy speeches and repeating the same motion may be treated as disorder, which can lead to removal from the courtroom or a fine on the representative — [RAPSI 27.12.2021](https://rapsinews.ru/publications/20211227/307627606.html)

#### T1. Challenging the representative's power of attorney (ваколатларни текшириш / оспаривание полномочий представителя)
- **What**: The opponent opens the hearing by attacking the other side's representative. Typical grounds: the power of attorney (ishonchnoma) is not signed by the director or another person authorised by the charter; the seal is missing although the entity has one; the document has expired; the representative is not an advocate yet acts professionally; the powers do not cover settlement, waiver of the claim or an appeal.
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 61 part 4 limits contractual representatives to: advocates, employees of the legal entity, NGO representatives, co-participants, and "other persons admitted by the court". The final sentence of art. 61 says: "only advocates may engage in professional activity as a representative in court."
  - Art. 62 part 2: a power of attorney from a legal entity must be signed by its head or a person authorised by the founding documents, and sealed if the entity has a seal. Part 3: an advocate's powers are confirmed as the law provides. Part 4: a non-advocate contractual representative must also show a document confirming that status.
  - Art. 63 part 1: the representative may do all procedural acts except the special ones. The special acts are: signing the claim, referring the case to arbitration, waiving or admitting the claim, changing its subject or basis, a settlement or mediation agreement, delegation, appealing, filing for review on newly discovered circumstances, demanding enforcement, and receiving money or property. Part 2: each special power must be expressly stated in the power of attorney.
  - Art. 64: the court must check powers and records the documents in the minutes. Part 3: if the documents are missing or non-compliant, the court refuses to recognise the powers, and this is noted in the minutes.
  - Art. 165 part 5 item 2: the presiding judge checks powers at the opening of the hearing.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **UZ consequence for claims**: Art. 107 item 3: a claim signed by an unauthorised person, or with no position or name of the signatory, is left without consideration. The cross-reference is art. 155 part 1 item 2 (return of the claim). [UZ-VERIFIED] — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 59 (representatives; since 2019 a higher legal education or academic degree is required, with exceptions), art. 61 (formalisation of powers; part 4 requires the diploma), art. 62 part 2 (special powers), art. 63 (the court checks powers). RAPSI reports the VS RF Plenum position that a copy of the higher-legal-education document must be filed if it is not already in the case file — [RAPSI](https://rapsinews.ru/publications/20211227/307627606.html). Pravo.ru reports that the Plenum No. 46 clarifications allowed an uncertified copy of the diploma — [Pravo.ru news 237850](https://pravo.ru/news/237850/)
- **Example line (lease debt with penalty)**: "Ҳурматли суд! The respondent objects to admitting the plaintiff's representative. The power of attorney was signed by the deputy director, and the LLC charter gives only the director the right to issue powers of attorney. Under EPC arts. 62 and 64, we ask the court to refuse to recognise these powers."
- **Stage**: Opening of the hearing (art. 165 part 5 item 2), before the merits. It also comes up at the settlement stage: "your PoA has no settlement power under art. 63."
- **Signals**: The opponent asks to see the original PoA; asks who signed it and under which charter clause; checks the date; asks "are you an advocate or an employee?"
- **Counter**: Bring the original PoA, the charter or a board decision showing the signatory's authority, and an advocate's order (ордер) or employment proof. If the challenge succeeds, the director can appear in person (art. 61 part 1: the entity's organs act within their charter powers) or ratify. Ask for a break (art. 172, up to 3 days) or an adjournment (art. 171: the representative cannot attend for a valid reason; the law also lists a party needing time to present evidence) to fix the documents. Before any settlement, check that the PoA contains the special powers.
- **Student mistakes**: A general PoA without the art. 63 special powers, so the student cannot sign a settlement or a claim waiver on the spot. A non-advocate acting professionally (art. 61, final sentence). Not bringing the original. Arguing the merits after the powers were refused.
- **Abuse line**: Repeating the same powers objection after the court has decided it, or challenging an obviously valid PoA just to provoke an adjournment. UZ has no direct sanction. The counter-argument is art. 42 part 2 (good faith) plus a request that the court decide immediately and continue. RU: art. 41 part 2, art. 159 part 5.

#### T2. Pleading the limitation period (даъво муддатини қўллаш ҳақида арз / заявление о пропуске срока исковой давности)
- **What**: The defendant pleads that the 3-year limitation period has expired for some or all instalments (for example old lease months or old penalty periods).
- **UZ substantive rule [UZ-CC-VERIFIED]**:
  - CC art. 150: the general limitation period is 3 years.
  - CC art. 153: a claim is accepted regardless of limitation; "limitation is applied by the court only on the application of a party to the dispute, made before the court issues its decision"; expiry is grounds to dismiss.
  - CC art. 154: the period runs from the day the person learned or should have learned of the violation; for obligations with a fixed due date it runs from the end of the performance period.
  - CC art. 157 (interruption): cited via Plenum No. 282 p.13.
  — [lex.uz Civil Code](https://lex.uz/docs/111181)
- **UZ Plenum No. 282 of 19.06.2015 (Higher Economic Court; amended by Supreme Court Plenum No. 17 of 19.05.2018, No. 33 of 20.11.2023 and No. 39 of 16.12.2024)** — [lex.uz/docs/2712570](https://lex.uz/docs/2712570):
  - p.3: a contractual or legal **pretenziya period is not a limitation period**; it is pretrial procedure.
  - p.6: an agreement changing limitation periods is void (CC 152).
  - p.7: an expired period is not grounds to refuse or return the claim.
  - p.8: the application may be oral or written; an oral one must be discussed and recorded in the minutes.
  - p.8.1: a reply (отзыв) that mentions expiry together with other arguments for dismissal counts as a limitation application.
  - p.8.2: only the **defendant** may plead it; pleas by other participants are disregarded (the original plaintiff may plead it against a counterclaim).
  - p.8.3: with several defendants, limitation applies only for the one who pleaded.
  - p.8.4: when several claims are joined, only time-barred claims fail.
  - p.8.5: if the plea is rejected, the court issues a ruling, which is appealable only with the final act.
  - p.9: the plea is allowed **only before the first-instance final act**. A plea made on appeal, cassation or revision is rejected, unless the higher court hears the case under first-instance rules; after a remand it may be made again.
  - p.13-13.3: interruption is caused by filing the claim or by acts acknowledging the debt: partial payment, **signing a reconciliation act (акт сверки)**, accepting a payment demand, letters admitting the debt. These must be done by an **authorised representative under CC ch.10** and **within** the limitation period; an acknowledgement after expiry does not revive the claim if the defendant pleads limitation.
  - p.16-16.3: restoration of the period for a valid reason. The court must examine the reasons once limitation is pleaded. Restoration is decided **only by the first instance**, and the reason must have arisen in the last 6 months of the period.
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: GK RF art. 196 (3 years), art. 199 part 2 (only on a party's application before the decision), art. 200, art. 203 (interruption by acknowledgement), art. 205 (restoration **only for individuals**, not for legal entities or entrepreneurs in business matters). VS RF Plenum No. 43 of 29.09.2015 — [vsrf.ru/documents/all/8453](https://vsrf.ru/documents/all/8453/). From memory, not re-read: p.10 (the plea is made by a party; a third party's plea does not count except under recourse logic); p.11 (the plea is made at first instance; on appeal only if the court moved to first-instance rules); p.20 (acts acknowledging debt; a reconciliation act must be signed by an authorised person); p.12 (no restoration for legal entities).
- **UZ vs RU difference for the simulator**: In UZ, Plenum 282 p.16 lets the court restore the period for a business plaintiff for a valid reason. In RU, GK art. 205 does not allow this for legal entities. [UZ part verified; RU part RU-NOT-REFETCHED]
- **Example line**: "The respondent pleads limitation for the rent and penalty for January–October 2022 (CC art. 153 part 2). The claim was filed on 14.11.2025, so instalments due before 14.11.2022 are time-barred (CC arts. 150, 154 part 2)."
- **Stage**: Written reply (art. 156) or orally at any first-instance hearing up to the judges' retirement for deliberation. A strong AI tactic is to **hold it back until the final hearing**. This is lawful because the plea is allowed until the decision.
- **Signals**: Old claim periods; no reconciliation acts in the file; the respondent's reply mentions "муддат"; questions about when the plaintiff learned of the breach.
- **Counter**: (1) Prove interruption with reconciliation acts or partial payments signed by an authorised person (Plenum 282 p.13.3), and check the signatory's authority yourself. (2) Recalculate the start date per instalment (CC 154 part 2). (3) In UZ, file for restoration with evidence (Plenum 282 p.16), at first instance only. (4) Ask for the plea to be recorded in the minutes and limited to the defendant who made it (p.8.3). (5) On appeal, object that a new limitation plea is inadmissible (p.9).
- **Student mistakes**: Raising limitation for the first time on appeal. Assuming the court applies it on its own initiative. Pleading it on behalf of a third party. Treating the pretenziya period as limitation. Not checking that the reconciliation act was signed by an authorised person.
- **Abuse line**: Pleading limitation is never abuse in itself; it is the defendant's right. A late plea is lawful. The plaintiff's counter is evidence of interruption, not a complaint about lateness.

#### T3. Demanding originals or certified copies of documents (асл нусхани талаб қилиш / требование оригиналов документов)
- **What**: The opponent demands the original of the contract, the delivery note (yuk xati / ТТН), the acceptance act or the reconciliation act, and argues that a copy proves nothing. This is often a set-up for a falsification claim or for the conflicting-copies rule.
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 75 part 2: written evidence is filed as an original or a duly certified copy.
  - Art. 75 part 3: originals are produced at the court's request where the law requires an original "and in other necessary cases".
  - Art. 75 parts 4-5: foreign-language documents need certified translations; foreign documents must be legalised.
  - Art. 76: return of originals.
  - Art. 74 part 5: the court **cannot** treat a fact as proved by a copy alone if the original is lost or not filed, the parties' copies differ, and the original content cannot be established by other evidence.
  - Art. 80 part 1: the court may order an expert examination on its own initiative to check a **falsification application** ("тақдим этилган далилнинг қалбакилаштирилганлиги тўғрисидаги ариза").
  - I did not find a separate stand-alone "falsification" article in the EPC table of contents; it appears only inside art. 80.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 75 (written evidence; originals on demand), art. 71 part 6 (the identical conflicting-copies rule), art. 161 (statement of falsification: the court warns of criminal liability, may exclude the evidence with consent, and verifies it, for example by expert examination).
- **Example (supply)**: "The plaintiff filed a copy of the delivery note ТТН No. 117 with no 'received' stamp. We ask that the original be produced (EPC art. 75 part 3). If it is not produced and our copy differs, the fact of delivery is not proved (art. 74 part 5)."
- **Stage**: Preparation (art. 163) or the evidence-examination stage (art. 168).
- **Signals**: "Is this a scan?"; comparing stamps; the opponent files its own differing copy; mentions of "қалбаки".
- **Counter**: Bring the originals to every hearing; certify copies properly. If there are conflicting copies, rely on **other evidence**: payment orders, correspondence, reconciliation acts, warehouse logs, e-invoices (UZ e-faktura is digital evidence; art. 66 now lists рақамли далиллар, as amended by ZRU-1003 of 21.11.2024). Demand that a falsification claim be formalised, then move for an expert examination under art. 80 with the deposit paid by the movant (art. 117).
- **Student mistakes**: Relying only on scans; not reading art. 74 part 5; reacting emotionally to a "forgery" accusation instead of asking for an expert examination.
- **Abuse line**: A knowingly unfounded falsification claim made to delay. RU: art. 161 includes a warning of criminal liability; delay is covered by arts. 41 and 111. UZ: no specific sanction found. The art. 117 deposit requirement deters a cheap delay.

#### T4. Disputing relevance and admissibility of evidence (алоқадорлик ва мақбуллик / относимость и допустимость)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 66 part 2: evidence obtained in breach of law cannot be used.
  - Art. 71: the court accepts only relevant evidence; irrelevant evidence is not added to the file, and the rejection is noted in the judicial act.
  - Art. 72: facts that law requires to be proved by specific evidence cannot be proved by other evidence (text per ZRU-683 of 21.04.2021).
  - Art. 74 part 2: each item is assessed for relevance, admissibility and reliability; the totality is assessed for sufficiency. Part 4: no evidence has predetermined force.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 64 part 3, art. 67 (relevance), art. 68 (admissibility), art. 71 (assessment).
- **Example (construction acceptance)**: "The contractor's witness statements cannot prove acceptance of works. Acceptance must be documented in an act signed by both parties. Under art. 72, other evidence is inadmissible for this fact." The student counters: "The Civil Code allows a unilateral act if the customer refused acceptance without reasons; we have the cover letter and the post-office receipt." (The CC article on unilateral acts was not verified in this session.)
- **Stage**: Art. 168 examination; also in debates (art. 173).
- **Signals**: "Мақбул эмас", "алоқадор эмас"; the opponent objects to attaching a document.
- **Counter**: State what fact the item proves (relevance, art. 71). Identify the statutory form, if any (art. 72). Ask that a ruling be recorded (art. 169: motions are decided after hearing the others, and the conclusions may be stated in the final act).
- **Student mistakes**: Filing large irrelevant bundles; not linking each exhibit to an element of the claim.

#### T5. Late evidence and new arguments (кечиктириб тақдим этилган далиллар / позднее раскрытие доказательств, «процессуальная засада»)
- **What**: The opponent files a bundle or a new legal theory at the last hearing to surprise the other side or force an adjournment.
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**: This is the key Uzbek disclosure rule.
  - Art. 68 part 3: each participant must **disclose its evidence to the others before the hearing begins or within the time the court sets**.
  - Art. 68 part 4: participants may rely **only on evidence the others were acquainted with in advance**.
  - Art. 68 part 5: if a party withholds evidence the court requested and does not produce it by the deadline, its contents are **presumed to be against that party and deemed admitted**.
  - Art. 156: the written reply with supporting documents and proof of sending copies must be filed so that it arrives before the hearing, **no later than 20 days** from the ruling on preparation.
  - Art. 170 part 1: if no reply or additional evidence is filed, the case is decided on the existing materials.
  - Art. 174: the court may resume examination of evidence during or after debates if it considers new evidence necessary.
  - Appeal: art. 276 says the appeal court **may examine new evidence and establish new facts**, but new **claims** not raised at first instance are not accepted. Art. 274 part 3: on appeal, the rules on changing the claim, counterclaims and joining third parties do not apply.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 65 part 3 (disclosure before the hearing or within the court's deadline), art. 65 part 4 (reliance only on disclosed evidence), art. 66 part 1 (filing), art. 131 (reply/отзыв; if not filed, art. 131 part 4 and art. 156 part 1 allow deciding on the file), art. 268 part 2 (on appeal, new evidence only if it could not be filed at first instance for valid reasons). **Key difference**: Russian appeal is restrictive; Uzbek art. 276 is permissive on new evidence.
- **Example (lease)**: At the 3rd hearing the respondent hands over 40 pages of "acts of defects in the premises" and asks to set off rent. Student: "The respondent did not disclose this evidence before the hearing (EPC art. 68 part 3). Under art. 68 part 4 it cannot rely on it. If the court admits it, we ask for a break of up to 3 days (art. 172) to study it, and we ask the court to note in the minutes that the delay is the respondent's fault."
- **Stage**: Any hearing, typically the last one, or debates (a "new argument" in the closing speech).
- **Signals**: The opponent arrives with a thick folder; copies were not sent to you; "we will clarify in debates".
- **Counter**: Cite art. 68 parts 3-4. Ask the court to refuse to attach the evidence or, if it is admitted, to give time (art. 172 break rather than an art. 171 adjournment, so the case stays on track). If the opponent ignored a court request for evidence, invoke the art. 68 part 5 presumption and the art. 69 fine. In debates, object that new facts cannot be argued without examined evidence (see T13).
- **Student mistakes**: Agreeing to an adjournment, which helps the delaying party. Not asking for an adverse inference. Filing their own reply after the 20-day deadline (art. 156).
- **Abuse line**: Systematic sandbagging. UZ: art. 42 part 2 (good faith), art. 68 parts 4-5, art. 69 fines, art. 118 (costs follow the result). RU: art. 65 part 5 / art. 111 part 2 (costs on the abuser) and art. 159 part 5. [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]

#### T6. Adjournment as a delay tool (суд муҳокамасини кейинга қолдириш / ходатайство об отложении)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**: Art. 171. Adjournment is allowed "in unexpected and extraordinary circumstances" where the case cannot be heard, including:
  - a party's request for help to settle;
  - a duly notified participant who justifies their absence, where the case cannot be heard without them;
  - the representative's absence for a valid reason;
  - the need to present additional evidence or obtain it from third parties;
  - a failed video link;
  - no proof of notification;
  - replacing an improper defendant, a successor, or joining a second defendant or third party.
  **Limits**: each adjournment is at most **10 days**, within the statutory hearing period, and there may be **no more than three adjournments**. Witnesses who appeared may be questioned before adjourning. Art. 172: a **break** of up to 3 days; evidence examined before the break is not re-examined even if representatives change; participants are deemed notified. Art. 165 part 3: the first hearing is held within 20 days of the preparation ruling. — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891); further guidance: Higher Economic Court Plenum No. 283 of 19.06.2015 on procedural time limits (referenced in the art. 69 and 171 commentary; not read in full).
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 158 (part 3: a representative's absence for a valid reason gives the court discretion, not an obligation; part 5: adjournment up to one month), art. 163 (break up to 5 days), art. 152 (overall time limit).
- **Example**: "Our representative is in another hearing, and we ask for an adjournment (art. 171)." Student: "The respondent was duly notified (art. 170 part 3); the director can appear himself; a busy representative is not an 'unexpected and extraordinary circumstance'. Two adjournments have already been granted at the respondent's request; the limit is three (art. 171 part 2)."
- **Signals**: A motion without supporting documents; the representative changes before each hearing; medical certificates without dates; repeated "settlement talks" with no draft.
- **Counter**: Object on the merits of the art. 171 grounds. Count the adjournments. Ask for a 3-day break instead. Ask to hear the witnesses who appeared now (art. 171). Ask the court to decide on the existing materials (art. 170).
- **Student mistakes**: Agreeing out of politeness; not counting the adjournments; filing their own adjournment request and thereby using up the limit.
- **Abuse line**: RU: art. 159 part 5 refusal and art. 111 part 2 costs. UZ: the statutory caps plus art. 42 part 2. There is no specific fine for an unfounded adjournment request in the UZ EPC (none found).

#### T7. Requesting an expert examination to delay (экспертиза тайинлаш ҳақида илтимоснома / ходатайство о назначении экспертизы)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 80 part 1: the court appoints an expert examination on a participant's motion or with their consent; on its own initiative where law or contract requires it, to check a falsification claim, or for an additional examination.
  - Art. 80 part 2: the court defines the questions, participants may propose questions, and the court must give reasons for rejecting them.
  - Art. 80 part 3: participants may propose experts or institutions, challenge an expert, add questions, give explanations to the expert, read the report and ask for additional or repeat examinations.
  - Art. 82: additional and repeat examinations.
  - Art. 86: the expert's report.
  - **Art. 117: the party that requests an expert, specialist, witness or translator must deposit the amount in advance to the court's deposit account**; if both parties request one, they share it equally.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891); Higher Economic Court Plenum No. 261 of 20.06.2014 on expertise (pp. 3, 6, 7, 8.3, 14, 20, cited in the code commentary; not read in full).
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK arts. 82-87, art. 108 (deposit), art. 144 part 1 (suspension pending the examination). VAS Plenum No. 23 of 04.04.2014 p.22: without the deposit the court should refuse the motion — [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/)
- **Example (construction)**: The customer refuses to pay and asks for a forensic construction examination of the volume and quality of the work, without proposing an institution, listing questions or depositing the fee.
- **Signals**: The motion has no candidate expert, no questions and no deposit receipt; the questions concern undisputed facts; it is filed at a late stage.
- **Counter**: Demand the deposit first (art. 117). Show that the facts are already proved (signed КС-2/acceptance act, no claims at acceptance) so no special knowledge is needed (art. 80 part 1). Propose your own questions and institution so you control the scope. Ask the court to set a short deadline for the report.
- **Student mistakes**: Not objecting at all; consenting to an open-ended question list; not proposing their own questions, and so losing the art. 80 part 2 safeguard.
- **Abuse line**: RU: refusal under art. 159 part 5 and art. 111. UZ: deposit requirement plus refusal on relevance (art. 71).

#### T8. Motions to involve third parties (учинчи шахсни жалб этиш / привлечение третьих лиц)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 47: third parties with independent claims may enter before the first-instance decision; if they enter after the hearing began, **the hearing restarts from the beginning**; they are exempt from the pretenziya requirement.
  - Art. 48: third parties without independent claims may join on either side if the case may affect their rights or obligations toward a party. They join on a party's motion or the court's initiative, by ruling. If they join after the hearing began, the case is **heard from the beginning**. They cannot change the claim, settle or file a counterclaim (text per ZRU-531 of 20.03.2019).
  - Art. 171: joining a second defendant or third party is grounds for adjournment.
  - Art. 274 part 3: not available on appeal.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 50 and art. 51 (the hearing restarts from the beginning), art. 266 part 3 (none on appeal).
- **Example (supply)**: The buyer moves to join the carrier and the end customer as third parties, "because the goods were damaged in transit".
- **Signals**: Several parties named at once; no explanation of how their rights would be affected; a late stage. Practitioners describe "multiple motions to join third parties" as a typical delay tactic — [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/)
- **Counter**: Prepare a written objection in advance for each likely candidate (practitioner tip by Евгений Зубков, ИНФРАЛЕКС, in the same Pravo.ru article). Argue that the judgment will not affect their rights toward either party (art. 48 part 1). Point out the restart consequence and the delay.
- **Student mistakes**: Agreeing and losing months; confusing a third party with a co-defendant.

#### T9. Counterclaim (қарши даъво / встречный иск)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**: Art. 160: the counterclaim may be filed **until the decision**. Conditions: it is aimed at set-off; or granting it excludes the original claim in full or in part; or the claims are interconnected and joint hearing leads to a timely and correct resolution. Art. 161: the usual filing rules apply (state fee, pretenziya where required, art. 149/151 attachments). Art. 274 part 3: not on appeal. The Plenum 282 p.8.2 link: the original plaintiff may plead limitation against the counterclaim. — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891); [Plenum 282](https://lex.uz/docs/2712570)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 132 (the same three conditions; filing before the final judicial act). The art. 159 part 5 and art. 132 counter for late counterclaims is described in [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/)
- **Example (lease debt)**: At the final hearing the tenant files a counterclaim for recovery of the cost of "unauthorised improvements" (inseparable improvements) and for invalidity of the penalty clause.
- **Signals**: Filed at the last hearing; requires completely new evidence; no pretenziya was sent first.
- **Counter**: Argue that none of the art. 160 conditions is met, or that joint hearing will delay rather than speed up the case. Check the art. 161 formalities: state fee, pretenziya where required (art. 107 item 5), proof of sending. The filer can still sue separately, so refusal does not prejudice them. Plead limitation against the counterclaim if needed.
- **Student mistakes**: Not checking whether pretrial procedure applies to the counterclaim; not pleading limitation against it.

#### T10. Recusal motions (рад қилиш / отвод судье)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 20 grounds: prior participation as a judge (where a repeat role is not allowed); prior participation as an arbitrator, prosecutor, expert, specialist, translator, clerk, representative or witness; being a relative of a participant or representative; direct or indirect personal interest "or other circumstances causing doubt about impartiality"; being a relative of another judge on the panel.
  - Art. 22 part 2: the motion must be **reasoned and made before the merits begin**. Part 3: later only if the grounds became known later. Part 4: **a repeat recusal by the same person on the same grounds is not allowed, and if made is not considered**.
  - Art. 23: a single judge's recusal is decided by the court chairman, or by the same judge in a one-judge court; a panel decides by majority without the challenged judge, and a tie means the judge is recused; a ruling is issued.
  - Art. 24: if granted, the case goes to another panel or court.
  - Art. 165 part 5 item 3: the judge explains the right to seek recusal at the opening.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 21 (grounds), art. 24 (procedure: before the merits; no repeat on the same grounds), art. 25 (decision; since 2019 a single judge's recusal is decided by the court chairman or deputy, or by a panel).
- **Example**: "We recuse the judge because at the last hearing she 'pressured' us to settle." Student: "Promoting settlement is the judge's statutory duty (art. 163 part 1 item 8). This is not an art. 20 ground. The motion is not reasoned under art. 22 part 2, and it is late because the merits have begun."
- **Signals**: Recusal immediately after an adverse procedural ruling; vague "doubts"; the same ground repeated.
- **Counter**: Point to the missing art. 20 ground, the lateness (art. 22 parts 2-3) and the repetition bar (art. 22 part 4), and ask the court to continue.
- **Student mistakes**: Filing recusal out of frustration with rulings (dissatisfaction with rulings is not a ground); filing it late.
- **Abuse line**: Repeated recusal. The UZ statutory consequence is non-consideration (art. 22 part 4). RU: Plenum and practice treat repeated groundless recusal as abuse (arts. 41 and 111) [RU-NOT-REFETCHED].

#### T11. "Prove every element" and the burden of proof (исботлаш мажбурияти / бремя доказывания)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Art. 68 part 1: each participant proves the circumstances it relies on for its claims and objections.
  - Art. 68 part 2: in cases on legal-influence measures, the controlling body bears the burden.
  - Art. 67: proof consists of collecting, examining, verifying and assessing evidence.
  - Art. 69: evidence is presented by the participants; the court may suggest additional evidence; a party unable to obtain evidence may ask the court to demand it, stating which fact it proves, its features and location; a holder who fails without valid reason is **fined**, and the fine does not relieve them of the duty to produce.
  - Art. 73: exemptions from proof: facts recognised as commonly known; facts established by a final economic-court decision between the same parties; final civil/administrative court decisions; criminal verdicts on whether acts occurred and by whom.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 65 part 1, art. 66 (parts 4-9: demanding evidence; part 9 fine), art. 69 (prejudice), art. 70 (admission of circumstances; part 3.1: circumstances not expressly disputed are deemed admitted).
- **Elements for typical claims (UZ)**: For lease debt with a penalty, the plaintiff proves the contract, the transfer of premises (acceptance act), the period, the amount due and the penalty clause and its calculation. The defendant proves payment or a defence. For a penalty reduction, the **debtor** must show disproportion (see T15). For fault-based liability, the debtor is presumed at fault and must prove it took all measures (UZ Plenum No. 163 p.12, citing CC art. 333): lack of funds, counterparty breaches or market shortages are **not** excuses — [Plenum 163, lex.uz/docs/1636812](https://lex.uz/docs/1636812)
- **Example**: The AI respondent says: "The plaintiff has not proved the premises were actually transferred; there is no acceptance act."
- **Counter**: Map each element to an exhibit before the hearing. Use art. 69 to make the court demand documents the opponent holds, then rely on the art. 68 part 5 presumption if they withhold them. Use art. 73 prejudice from earlier cases between the same parties.
- **Student mistakes**: Assuming the court will collect evidence. Saying "it's obvious" instead of citing art. 73. Forgetting that the defendant must prove its own objections (art. 68 part 1).

#### T12. Shifting the burden with presumptions (тахминлар / презумпции)
- **UZ tools [UZ-VERIFIED]**: Art. 68 part 5 (withheld evidence is presumed against the holder and deemed admitted). Art. 73 (prejudice). Presumption of the debtor's fault for breach (Plenum 163 p.12 / CC 333). Art. 172 part 6 (a participant notified before a break is deemed notified afterwards). — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891); [Plenum 163](https://lex.uz/docs/1636812)
- **RU [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 70 part 3.1 ("silence = admission"); the art. 66 part 9 fine; the burden-shifting doctrine in practice.
- **Counter to an opponent's presumption**: Show you were never formally requested by the court (art. 69 part 5 requires a ruling with a deadline), or that you notified the court of impossibility within the deadline (art. 69 part 7).

#### T13. Objecting to unexamined evidence or new facts in the closing (текширилмаган далилга асосланишга эътироз / возражение против ссылок на неисследованные доказательства в прениях)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**: Art. 173: debates start only after all evidence has been examined and the presiding judge has asked whether there are additional materials. In debates, participants "justify their position on the case". The plaintiff speaks first, then the third party with independent claims, then the defendant. Art. 174: if new circumstances or evidence must be examined, the court **resumes the examination** and then holds debates again. Art. 68 part 4: reliance only on evidence previously disclosed. Art. 168: the scope of examination. — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 164 (debates; part 3 says participants may not refer to circumstances the court did not ascertain or evidence it did not examine), art. 165 (resumption of examination).
- **Example**: In the closing speech the respondent says: "Moreover, the plaintiff's director admitted in a phone call that the goods were defective." Student: "Ҳурматли суд, this is not in the evidence; we ask the court to disregard it. If the respondent wants to rely on it, the court should resume examination under art. 174."
- **Counter**: Interrupt politely, only through the presiding judge. Then use the reply (реплика) in debates to rebut it.

#### T14. Pretrial claim (pretenziya) procedure as a defence (низони судгача ҳал қилиш (талабнома юбориш) тартиби / досудебный (претензионный) порядок)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - **Art. 107 item 5**: the court leaves the claim **without consideration** if the plaintiff did not follow the pretrial (talabnoma) procedure **where it is required for this category by law or by the parties' contract**. Items 5¹-5²: the same for mandatory mediation.
  - **Art. 118 part 4**: if the case arose because a participant breached the pretrial procedure (left a claim unanswered, failed to send requested documents), **the court may put the costs on that person regardless of the outcome**.
  - Art. 118 part 5: if the defendant pays voluntarily after the claim is filed, costs are on the defendant.
  - Art. 47 part 1: a third party with independent claims is exempt.
  - The pretrial requirement is also referenced in arts. 110, 141, 148, 149, 151 and 155 (claim contents and attachments, return).
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **UZ Plenum 282 p.3**: the pretenziya period is not a limitation period. Examples of statutory pretrial requirements: share buy-back (JSC law art. 41), railway carriage (Railway Charter p.152), air and road passenger carriage. — [Plenum 282](https://lex.uz/docs/2712570)
- **Note**: Whether the general law "On the contractual-legal basis of activity of business entities" still makes pretenziya mandatory for all B2B disputes is **not verified** here. The wording of art. 107 item 5 ("where provided by law or contract") suggests it is mandatory only where a law or the contract provides for it. Check the current law before you script this in the simulator.
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK art. 4 part 5: for disputes arising from civil-law relations, a claim may be filed after 30 calendar days from sending the pretenziya unless law or contract sets another period, with listed exceptions. Art. 128 / art. 129 part 1 item 5: return of the claim. Art. 148 part 1 item 2: leaving it without consideration. Art. 111 part 1: costs on a party that breached pretrial procedure. VS RF Plenum No. 18 of 22.06.2021 «О некоторых вопросах досудебного урегулирования споров…» covers, among other things, a defendant who uses the pretrial objection to delay while showing no intent to settle. **RAPSI summary**: sending the claim by messenger or email counts only if law or contract provides for it or it is the parties' established practice — [RAPSI](https://rapsinews.ru/publications/20211227/307627606.html)
- **Example (supply)**: "Clause 9.2 of the supply contract requires a written pretenziya with a 15-day response period. The plaintiff sent only an email to the manager. Under EPC art. 107 item 5, we ask that the claim be left without consideration."
- **Signals**: The respondent asks for the pretenziya and proof of delivery; argues that the email is not a valid channel under the contract; files its objection at the first hearing.
- **Counter**: Produce the pretenziya, the postal receipt or delivery proof in the contractual form, and evidence that the response period had expired. If the respondent ignored the pretenziya, ask for costs on it under art. 118 part 4 even if it wins on another point. Argue that leaving the claim without consideration is pointless when the respondent clearly does not intend to settle. That argument is drawn from RU Plenum 18 [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]; no UZ Plenum equivalent was found.
- **Student mistakes**: Treating a phone call as a pretenziya; not keeping proof; not checking the contract's pretrial clause; confusing it with limitation.

#### T15. Penalty reduction: first instance only, and only with evidence (неустойкани камайтириш / снижение неустойки по ст. 333 ГК РФ)
- **UZ substantive rule [UZ-CC-VERIFIED]**: CC art. 326: if the penalty is clearly disproportionate to the consequences of breach, the court may reduce it, taking into account the degree of performance, the parties' property status and the creditor's interests; "in exceptional cases" the court may reduce it with regard to both parties' interests. — [lex.uz Civil Code](https://lex.uz/docs/111181)
- **UZ Plenum No. 163 of 15.06.2007** (Higher Economic Court, on property liability; amended by Supreme Court Plenum No. 17 of 2018 and No. 39 of 16.12.2024) — [lex.uz/docs/1636812](https://lex.uz/docs/1636812):
  - p.4: under CC art. 326 the court considers the degree of performance, the property status and the creditor's interests. **The penalty cannot be reduced below the interest under CC art. 327**. Contractually raising a statutory penalty (CC art. 263 part 2) is not in itself grounds for reduction.
  - p.11: CC 327 interest may also be reduced by analogy with art. 326 via art. 335.
  - p.12: the debtor's fault is presumed.
- **UZ procedural consequence [UZ-VERIFIED]**: EPC art. 118 part 6: if a penalty claim was justified but the court reduced it, costs are put on the defendant **based on the unreduced amount**. The plaintiff is not penalised in costs for the reduction. — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891)
- **UZ case practice found** (Higher Economic Court rulings collected on lex.uz): No. 10-0904/8403 of 20.10.2009 ("if the penalty is clearly disproportionate…") — [lex.uz/docs/2836258](https://lex.uz/docs/2836258); No. 10-1312/3177 of 26.08.2013 ("in exceptional cases…") — [lex.uz/docs/2882730](https://lex.uz/docs/2882730); No. 10-1021/3457(17) of 12.07.2010 ("the minimum penalty cannot be lower than the CC 327 interest") — [lex.uz/docs/2831719](https://lex.uz/docs/2831719). I identified these from their lex.uz headings only and did not read the full texts.
- **Whether UZ allows reduction on the court's own initiative**: The CC 326 wording ("суд вправе") and Plenum 163 p.4 do not expressly require the debtor's application. Unlike RU, I found no UZ text limiting reduction to a debtor's application at first instance. Treat "first instance only, with evidence" as **RU law**; in UZ the AI opponent should still raise it at first instance with evidence, because appellate review is of legality and validity (art. 276).
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: GK RF art. 333 part 1. For a commercial debtor, reduction is possible **only on the debtor's application** and only in exceptional cases where the debtor proves clear disproportion (VS RF Plenum No. 7 of 24.03.2016, pp. 71-73, 75). The application must be made at first instance; on appeal it is possible only if the court moved to first-instance rules (p.72). Increased contractual rates, the low rate of the key rate or the creditor's benefit are relevant factors; the debtor's lack of money is not a ground.
- **Example (lease)**: Rent debt of 120 mln UZS; the contract penalty is 0.5% per day, accrued 96 mln UZS. Respondent: "We ask for the penalty to be reduced under CC art. 326. We have paid 70% of the debt. Our financial statements show losses. The penalty equals 80% of the principal and exceeds CC 327 interest many times over."
- **Counter (plaintiff)**: Insist on evidence of disproportion. Argue that reduction cannot go below the CC 327 floor (Plenum 163 p.4). Argue that a business party knowingly accepted the rate. Ask for costs on the full amount (art. 118 part 6).
- **Student mistakes (defence)**: Asking without evidence; asking only on appeal; asking for reduction to zero; citing hardship (lack of funds is no excuse, Plenum 163 p.12).

#### T16. Settlement offers as pressure (келишув битими / мировое соглашение как средство давления)
- **UZ rule [UZ-VERIFIED]**:
  - Arts. 130-134.
  - Art. 131: a settlement may be concluded in any claim case at **any stage, including enforcement**; a mediation agreement only at first instance before the court retires to deliberate; the settlement takes effect only once the court approves it.
  - Art. 163 part 1 item 8: the judge takes measures to reconcile the parties.
  - Art. 171: a party's request for help to settle is grounds for adjournment.
  - Art. 63: a representative needs special power to settle.
  — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/docs/3523891); Higher Economic Court Plenum No. 204 of 18.12.2009 on approving settlements (cited in the code commentary; not read).
- **RU rule [RU-NOT-REFETCHED]**: APK arts. 138-142 (settlement at any stage, approved by the court), art. 158 part 2 (adjournment on a joint request for settlement), art. 62 (special powers); VS RF Plenum No. 50 of 18.10.2012 on conciliation procedures.
- **Tactic**: The opponent offers a "settlement" at the last minute with an expiring deadline ("only today"), or uses settlement talks to obtain an adjournment (art. 171) and then never signs.
- **Signals**: No written draft; the offer depends on dropping the penalty entirely; a request to adjourn "for negotiations" without a timetable.
- **Counter**: Ask for a written draft in court. Agree only to a break (art. 172), not an adjournment, or to an adjournment with a fixed short date. Check the opponent's power to settle (art. 63). Never concede facts orally during talks, because what is said in the minutes counts as explanations (art. 66).
- **Abuse line**: RU practitioners list "using conciliation procedures without the aim of reconciliation" as a typical delay tactic — [Pravo.ru review «как не дать затянуть процесс»](https://pravo.ru/review/view/144989/) (from the search summary only; the page was not opened).

#### T17. Other tactics found in the code and practice (short)
- **Arbitration clause objection (ҳакамлик келишуви)**: Art. 107 item 2: the claim is left without consideration only if the defendant objects **no later than its first statement on the merits**. A student defendant who argues the merits first loses this objection. [UZ-VERIFIED] RU: APK art. 148 part 1 item 5 [RU-NOT-REFETCHED].
- **Lis pendens (айни бир низо)**: Art. 107 item 1: the same parties, subject and grounds pending in another court. [UZ-VERIFIED]
- **Bank-collection precondition**: Art. 107 item 4: a debt that must be collected via a bank under law or contract, where the plaintiff did not apply to the bank. [UZ-VERIFIED]
- **Holding back documents the court asked for**: Art. 69 fine plus the art. 68 part 5 presumption (see T11-T12). [UZ-VERIFIED]
- **Empty envelope / partial service of documents**: The counter is a notarial protocol of opening the envelope (Рустам Курмаев) — [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/). UZ: art. 156 part 1 requires proof of sending copies with the reply.
- **Late third-party appeal with restoration of the deadline (RU)**: The counter is written objections to removing the case from the appellate calendar — [Pravo.ru](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/)

## Key Question 3: Russian advocacy literature on representative tactics (тактика представителя) and courtroom rhetoric — practical tips

### Takeaway
Russian practitioner literature, mainly Pravo.ru round-ups, treats delay as the dominant opponent tactic. The listed forms are: repeated or groundless motions, late counterclaims, multiple third-party joinders, expert-examination motions without a deposit, fake "conciliation", baseless jurisdiction-transfer motions, late appeals with restoration of the deadline, and incomplete service. The recommended counters are to anticipate them with pre-written objections, cite APK art. 159 part 5 and art. 111, record bad faith in the minutes, and use notarial protocols. The in-depth textbook literature on «судебная риторика» was not reached in this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Tactic and counter pairs with named practitioners** — [Pravo.ru, «Злоупотребления процессуальными правами: как отбиться от них в суде», author Максим Вараксин](https://pravo.ru/story/231471/):
  - Multiple third-party joinder motions → prepare reasoned written objections in advance (Евгений Зубков, ИНФРАЛЕКС).
  - Expert examination without candidates, questions or deposit → cite VAS Plenum 23 p.22 (Маргарита Шульгина, Delcredere).
  - Empty envelopes or partial service → notarial protocol of opening the envelope (Рустам Курмаев).
  - A forged "counter-contract" reversing the parties' roles → prompt falsification statement and expert examination; the forgery was confirmed (Дмитрий Якушев, АБ «Городисский и партнеры»).
  - Late counterclaim → APK art. 132 conditions, art. 159 part 5, and the filer can sue separately (Зубков).
  - Baseless jurisdiction-transfer motions → separate-file appellate procedure (Зубков).
  - Late third-party appeal with restoration of the deadline → written objection to removing the case from the appellate calendar (Курмаев).

### Inferences
- For the LexSim AI opponent, the "pressure" tactics that are most realistic and also lawful are: (1) the powers challenge at the opening, (2) the limitation plea held back to the end, (3) a document-original demand followed by a falsification hint, (4) a last-hearing evidence dump, (5) adjournment and expert-examination requests, (6) a third-party or counterclaim cascade, (7) a pretenziya objection at the first hearing, (8) an evidence-free penalty-reduction request, and (9) a "settle today" offer. Each maps to a verified UZ EPC counter above.
- Teaching point on rhetoric: Uzbek courtroom etiquette (art. 167: «Ҳурматли суд!», standing, addressing only the court) turns interruptions by the opponent into an order issue for the presiding judge to handle (warning, then removal). Students should object **through the court**, not directly to the opponent.
