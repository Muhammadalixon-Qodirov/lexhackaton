## Q1. Uzbekistan: Law on Advocacy, the Advocates' Ethics Rules, the Economic Procedure Code (IPK), and courtroom order

### Takeaway
Uzbek law draws the line in four places. (a) IPK Art. 42(2): every participant must use procedural rights in good faith ("insofli ravishda"). (b) IPK Art. 167: courtroom decorum, with a warning first and removal from the courtroom on a repeat breach. (c) IPK Arts. 125-126: court fines, but only "in cases provided by this Code". (d) The Advocates' Ethics Rules (2013 edition): no deliberate delay (p.54), respect for the court and participants (p.55), objections in correct form (p.56), no demeaning or insulting statements (p.11), no inducing false testimony (p.48). The IPK has no general "abuse of procedural rights" article with its own sanction; I searched the full text for "suiiste'mol" and found no match. Abuse is sanctioned indirectly: costs (Art. 118), repeated recusal is barred (Art. 22(4)), plus the specific fine provisions.

### Cited Findings

**Economic Procedure Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Iqtisodiy protsessual kodeks, 24.01.2018), [lex.uz/docs/-3523891](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)**
- **Art. 9.** Proceedings are adversarial and the parties have equal rights ("taraflarning tortishuvi va teng huquqliligi"). — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 42(1).** Participants may: read the case file; seek recusal; present evidence; take part in examining evidence; ask questions; file motions; give oral and written explanations; give their arguments on every issue; and object to the motions and arguments of other participants. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 42(2), verbatim.** "Ishda ishtirok etuvchi shaxslar ushbu Kodeksda nazarda tutilgan protsessual majburiyatlarga ega va ular oʻzlariga tegishli barcha protsessual huquqlardan insofli ravishda foydalanishi kerak." In English: participants bear the procedural duties set by the Code and must use all their procedural rights in good faith. The LexUZ commentary cross-references Arts. 22(1), 47, 48(2), 49(6), 50(1), 68(1), 69(3)-(7), 129, 136, 150, 167(1)-(5), 220 and 239(1). — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 42(3)** (added by Law OʻRQ-640 of 05.10.2020). In corporate disputes, parties may request from each other and from witnesses all documents relevant to the case, except state or other legally protected secrets. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 22(4).** The same person may not repeat a recusal application on the same grounds. This is an anti-abuse rule against serial recusal motions as a pressure or delay tactic. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 43(5).** The parties have equal procedural rights. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 68.** Each party must prove the circumstances it relies on (isbotlash majburiyati). — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 118 (allocation of costs).** Costs follow the share of claims satisfied (part 1). Part 4: if the case arose because a participant breached the mandatory pre-trial claim procedure (left a claim letter unanswered, or failed to send requested documents), the court may put the costs on that person regardless of the outcome. Part 5: if the defendant voluntarily satisfies the claim after filing, the defendant bears the costs. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 125 (court fines).** Fines are imposed only "in cases provided for by this Code". The LexUZ cross-references include Art. 58(3) (specialist) and Art. 69(8) (evidence). Maximum amounts: up to 50 BHM (base calculation amount) for citizens and up to 200 BHM for legal entities (wording per Law OʻRQ-586 of 03.12.2019). Fines on officials are paid from their personal funds and go to the republican budget. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 126 (procedure).** A fine on a person present at the hearing is decided at that hearing. A person who is absent must be notified. The fine is imposed by ruling (ajrim), which can be appealed, and the appeal does not suspend it. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 167 (courtroom order).**
  - Everyone rises when the judges enter and leave.
  - Participants address the court as "Hurmatli sud!" and speak standing, unless the presiding judge permits otherwise.
  - Photo, video and broadcast activity must not disrupt order.
  - **Part 4:** "Majlis vaqtida tartib buzilgan taqdirda, raislik qiluvchi tartibni buzgan shaxsni sud nomidan ogohlantiradi. Tartib takroran buzilgan taqdirda, mazkur shaxs raislik qiluvchining farmoyishi bilan sud majlisi zalidan chiqarib yuborilishi mumkin." In English: on a breach of order the presiding judge warns the person in the court's name; on a repeat breach the person may be removed by the presiding judge's order. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 65.** Judges, investigators, prosecutors and court staff may not act as representatives. Arbitrators and mediators in the same dispute may not either (mediators subject to an exception; the arbitrator rule was added by OʻRQ-1141, in force 24.07.2026). — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)
- **Art. 53(2).** Representatives in economic cases cannot be questioned as witnesses about what they learned while acting as representatives. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/-3523891)

**Law "On Advocacy" (Advokatura toʻgʻrisida, No. 349-I of 27.12.1996), [lex.uz/docs/-54503](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)**
- **Art. 7(1).** In professional activity an advocate must comply with legislation, the "Advokatning kasb etikasi qoidalari" (Rules of Professional Ethics), advocate secrecy and the advocate's oath. The advocate must use only the means and methods of protection provided by law ("qonunda nazarda tutilgan vositalari hamda usullaridan foydalanishi shart"), per wording of OʻRQ-683 of 21.04.2021. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)
- **Art. 7(2).** Conflict-of-interest bars: the advocate may not act against current or former clients, or where the advocate previously acted as judge, prosecutor, expert, witness and similar roles. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)
- **Art. 14 (disciplinary liability).** Breaching the advocacy legislation, the Ethics Rules, secrecy or the oath leads to disciplinary measures. These are: a warning; suspension of the licence for up to 6 months; termination of the licence. Proceedings are opened by the qualification commission. The limitation period is 6 months from the misconduct (2 years for financial audit findings). — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)
- **Art. 16.** The licence can be terminated for systematic or single gross breach of the Ethics Rules, or for an act that "discredits the honour and dignity of the advocate and the reputation of the profession". — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)
- **Art. 10.** No one may interfere with an advocate's professional activity or exert any form of influence on the advocate while performing professional duties. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-54503)

**Law "On guarantees of advocacy and social protection of advocates" (No. 721-I of 25.12.1998), [lex.uz/acts/-29626](https://lex.uz/acts/-29626)**
- **Art. 3 (oath), verbatim.** "Oʻz kasbiy burchimni halol va vijdonan bajarishga, inson huquqlari va erkinliklarini hamisha himoya qilishga, advokatlik sirini saqlashga, Oʻzbekiston Respublikasining Konstitutsiyasi va qonunlariga qatʼiy rioya etishga tantanali qasamyod qilaman". In English: to perform professional duty honestly and conscientiously, and to strictly observe the Constitution and laws. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-29626)
- **Art. 4.** As a party, an advocate has rights equal to all other participants at every stage. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-29626)
- The same law makes liable anyone who: exerts influence on an advocate to obstruct the case or to force a position contrary to the client's lawful interest; threatens, insults or slanders an advocate; or uses force against an advocate. This is the mirror rule: pressure on counsel is itself unlawful. — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/acts/-29626)

**Rules of Professional Ethics of Advocates (Правила профессиональной этики адвокатов): Annex No. 8 to the decision of the II Conference of the Chamber of Advocates, 27.09.2013** — [nrm.uz](https://nrm.uz/contentf?doc=549534_pravila_professionalnoy_etiki_advokatov_%28prilojenie_n_8_k_resheniyu_ii_konferencii_palaty_advokatov_ruz_ot_27_09_2013_g_%29&products=1_vse_zakonodatelstvo_uzbekistana) **[secondary: fragments as rendered by a commercial database; full official text not retrieved]**
- p.7: breaching the Rules leads to disciplinary liability under the Law "On Advocacy".
- p.9: the advocate must be polite, tactful, honest, conscientious and principled ("вежливым, тактичным, предупредительным, честным, добросовестным, принципиальным").
- p.11, prohibited bullets, among them:
  - making statements that demean or insult the honour and dignity of other participants;
  - acting under outside pressure;
  - obtaining instructions with a "promise of a favourable outcome";
  - public statements that the client's guilt is proven when the client denies it.
- p.18 and p.36: presumption that documents are authentic. The advocate is not responsible for the authenticity of client-supplied documents if there was no information that they were forged.
- p.19 and p.21: duty to respect colleagues' honour, dignity and business reputation; no improper expressions about colleagues in legal documents or letters.
- p.26: the advocate may speak with the client's procedural opponent only with consent (wording truncated in source; in comparable codes the consent needed is that of the opponent's advocate).
- p.48: the advocate may not induce a victim, witness or other person to give false testimony.
- **p.54:** "Адвокат не вправе умышленно затягивать или нарушать сроки ... судебного разбирательства." In English: no deliberate delaying of proceedings.
- **p.55:** comply with law in court; "проявлять уважение к суду и другим участникам процесса" (show respect to the court and other participants).
- **p.56:** "Возражая против действий судьи и других участников процесса, адвокат должен делать это в корректной форме" (objections must be made in a correct, civil form).
- p.62-63: no liability for an advocate who acted on the Chamber's written guidance, and none for de minimis acts.
- An explicit "do not mislead the court" rule was **not found** in the retrieved fragments.
- Earlier editions: Rules adopted by the founding conference of the Chamber on 12.09.2008 ([cyberleninka](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/institutsionalnaya-nezavisimost-advokatury-v-uzbekistane-problemy-i-puti-ih-resheniya); 2008 text at [prg.kz](https://prg.kz/Document/?doc_id=30469209)). Point I.1.2 of the 2008 text: "Злоупотребление доверием несовместимо со статусом адвоката" (abuse of trust is incompatible with advocate status).
- In October 2023 the Chamber drafted amendments to the ethics rules restricting advocates' public and social-media comments. Advocates opposed them. Whether they were adopted is unknown. — [gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/oz/2023/10/23/lawyers/)

### Inferences
- **For LexSim, an "Uzbek-legal" opponent may:**
  - object, file motions, challenge evidence, and argue hard (Art. 42(1));
  - insist the claimant prove each element (Art. 68);
  - exploit the claimant's failure to follow pre-trial claim procedure (Art. 118(4)).
- **It may not:**
  - delay deliberately (Rules p.54);
  - insult or demean (Rules p.11, p.56);
  - induce false testimony (p.48);
  - file repeat recusal on the same grounds (IPK Art. 22(4));
  - disrupt order. The simulated judge should warn first and remove on a repeat breach (Art. 167(4)).
- **Mirror rule.** Because Uzbek law (Law 721-I) also protects the advocate *from* pressure, a simulated "threat to report you to the Chamber" or "threat of liability" used to coerce the student's position is outside legitimate tactics.

## Q2. Comparators: ABA Model Rules, UK BSB Handbook, Russian Code of Professional Ethics of Advocates

### Takeaway
All three systems share one core: never knowingly (UK: or recklessly) mislead the court; no frivolous positions; no tampering with evidence or witnesses; no conduct whose only purpose is to humiliate. The key negotiation carve-out is ABA Rule 4.1 Comment [2]: estimates of price or value and a party's settlement intentions ("puffing") are ordinarily not statements of material fact. Invented facts (such as a fake competing offer) are prohibited.

### Cited Findings

**UK Bar Standards Board Handbook (v5.0, in force 15.06.2026)** — [barstandardsboard.org.uk](https://www.barstandardsboard.org.uk/the-bsb-handbook.html?q=mislead)
- Core Duties:
  - CD1: observe your duty to the court in the administration of justice.
  - CD2: act in the client's best interests.
  - CD3: act with honesty and integrity.
  - CD4: maintain your independence.
  - CD5: do not diminish public trust and confidence in you or the profession.
- **rC3:** duty to the court to act with independence in the interests of justice.
  - .1 "you must not knowingly or recklessly mislead or attempt to mislead the court"
  - .2 "must not abuse your role as an advocate"
  - .3 take reasonable steps to avoid wasting the court's time
  - .4 ensure the court has all relevant decisions and legislative provisions, including adverse ones
  - .5 independence not compromised
- **rC4:** the duty to the client is subject to the duty to the court.
- **rC6.2:** do not call witnesses or put in witness statements you know or are instructed are untrue or misleading, unless you make clear to the court the true position.
- **rC7.1:** "must not make statements or ask questions merely to insult, humiliate or annoy a witness or any other person".
- **rC9:**
  - .1 must not knowingly or recklessly mislead anyone;
  - .2 must not draft documents containing (a) facts not supported by the client or instructions, (b) contentions not properly arguable, or (c) allegations of fraud without clear instructions and reasonably credible material.
- **gC4.2:** inadvertently misleading the court becomes "knowing" if you realise it later and fail to correct the position.
- Enforcement example: a disciplinary finding for *recklessly* misleading the court under rC3.1 — [BTAS report, Nov 2023](https://tbtas.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/hearings/137063/BOGLE-Approved-Report-of-Finding-and-Sanction-Nov-2023.pdf).

**ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct** — [americanbar.org Model Rules](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/model_rules_of_professional_conduct_table_of_contents/). The fetch was blocked (403), so the rule texts below are from prior knowledge, standard wording **[verify before quoting]**.
- **3.1:** no proceeding, issue or position unless there is a non-frivolous basis in law and fact, including a good-faith argument to change the law.
- **3.3(a):** must not knowingly (1) make a false statement of fact or law to a tribunal, or fail to correct one previously made; (2) fail to disclose directly adverse controlling authority not disclosed by opposing counsel; (3) offer evidence known to be false.
- **3.4:**
  - (a) no unlawful obstruction of access to evidence, and no altering, destroying or concealing it;
  - (b) no falsifying evidence or assisting false testimony;
  - (c) no knowing disobedience of tribunal rules;
  - (d) no frivolous discovery requests, and no failing to comply with proper ones;
  - (e) at trial, no alluding to matters unsupported by admissible evidence, and no asserting personal knowledge or personal opinion on credibility or culpability;
  - (f) no asking non-clients to withhold information (limited exceptions).
- **3.5:** no improper influence on the judge; no ex parte communication unless authorised; (d) no conduct intended to disrupt the tribunal.
- **4.1:** in representing a client, no knowingly false statement of material fact or law to a third person.
- **4.1 Comment [2]:** estimates of price or value, and a party's intentions about an acceptable settlement, are "ordinarily" not statements of material fact. "Ordinarily" was added by Ethics 2000, together with a reminder about criminal and tortious misrepresentation. A bluff of confidence in winning at trial is permitted; misrepresenting the existence or size of other offers is prohibited. — [Hinshaw-type summary via PLI](https://patentlawcenter.pli.edu/?p=4482); [ABA Formal Op. 06-439 summary](https://ezel.ai/ethics-opinions/aba/06-439-truthfulness-in-negotiation-and-mediation) **[secondary]**
- **4.4(a):** no means that have no substantial purpose other than to embarrass, delay or burden a third person.

**Russian Code of Professional Ethics of Advocates (КПЭА, adopted 31.01.2003, as amended to 18.04.2025)** — [legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/kodeks-professionalnoi-etiki-advokata-prinjat-vserossiiskim-sezdom/)
- **Art. 8(1):** "адвокат обязан честно, разумно, добросовестно, квалифицированно ... исполнять свои профессиональные обязанности" (the advocate must perform professional duties honestly, reasonably, conscientiously and competently). — [legalacts.ru](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/kodeks-professionalnoi-etiki-advokata-prinjat-vserossiiskim-sezdom/)
- **Art. 9(1)** (the advocate may not; excerpt and paraphrase):
  - (1) act against the client's lawful interests, including under outside pressure;
  - (3) state publicly that the client's guilt is proven when the client denies it;
  - (6) promise a favourable outcome, or obtain clients through personal ties with the court;
  - **(7) "допускать в процессе разбирательства дела высказывания, умаляющие честь и достоинство других участников разбирательства, даже в случае их нетактичного поведения"** (make statements during proceedings that demean other participants' honour and dignity, even if those participants behave tactlessly).
  - Art. 9(1)(7) is very relevant: provocation by the other side is no excuse.
  - Source: [legalacts.ru Art. 9](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/kodeks-professionalnoi-etiki-advokata-prinjat-vserossiiskim-sezdom/razdel-pervyi/statja-9/)
- **Art. 12:**
  - (1) the advocate must comply with procedural law and show respect to the court and the persons participating in the case;
  - (2) "возражая против действий (бездействия) судей и лиц, участвующих в деле, адвокат должен делать это в корректной форме и в соответствии с законом" (objections to the acts or omissions of judges and participants must be made in a correct form and in accordance with law). — [legalacts.ru Art. 12](https://legalacts.ru/kodeks/kodeks-professionalnoi-etiki-advokata-prinjat-vserossiiskim-sezdom/razdel-pervyi/statja-12/)
- Russian bar chambers often hear judges' complaints under Art. 12 (disrespect to the court). One practitioner argues that the court's own preceding provocation should be weighed. — [advgazeta.ru](https://www.advgazeta.ru/mneniya/vazhnoe-obstoyatelstvo/) **[opinion]**
- The Uzbek Rules p.55-56 closely mirror Russian Art. 12. This is a structural observation from comparing the texts.

### Inferences
- Legitimate pressure is aimed at **arguments, evidence, burden of proof, costs risk and time** (within the rules). Misconduct is aimed at **the person, the truth, the tribunal's independence, or procedure-as-weapon**. This is a usable classifier for the AI opponent's guardrail.
- Puffing (rule 10) is only safely applied in **negotiation or settlement** phases. In statements to the court the UK and ABA standards apply in full.

### Gaps
- No Uzbek rule found on duty to disclose adverse authority, or an express duty of candour to the court. The closest are the oath ("halol va vijdonan"), Rules p.9 and IPK Art. 42(2).
- ABA texts were not fetched directly (403). Quoted wording should be checked at americanbar.org.

## Q3. How advocacy programmes teach, assess and give feedback on handling pressure

### Takeaway
The dominant formative method is a structured critique. Hampel / ICCA: Headline, Playback, Reason, Remedy, Demonstration, Replay, focused on ONE point per performance. NITA's critique similarly starts with a headline and verbatim playback. This maps naturally onto an automated LexSim debrief. I found no rigorous empirical study measuring "performance under pressure" in legal simulations specifically; claims like "85% improvement" are unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Hampel method.** Six stages: Headline; Playback; Reason; Remedy; Demonstration; Replay. "ONE point only receives attention" after each exercise. — [saltyspeech blog (barrister)](https://saltyspeech.blogspot.com/2016/02/the-hampel-method.html); [NTU "Feedback to feed forward"](https://www.ntu.ac.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0031/295933/Feedback-to-feed-forward.pdf) **[secondary]**
- NTU frames the Reason, Remedy and Demonstration stages as "feed-forward" (forward-looking correction rather than only fault-finding). — [NTU](https://www.ntu.ac.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0031/295933/Feedback-to-feed-forward.pdf)
- The method was devised by Professor George Hampel KC (founding chair of the Australian Advocacy Institute). It is used in Inns of Court / ICCA training and was demonstrated for American Inns of Court training. — [Temple Law AER](https://law.temple.edu/aer/?p=4018); ICCA paper [icca.ac.uk PDF](https://www.icca.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Advocacy-Training-hampel-on-hampel.pdf) (image-based, not text-readable)
- **NITA-style review** (US Army advocacy guide): begins with a headline and a playback in which the reviewer reads back verbatim three examples of the issue. — [Advancing Advocacy, USAR OSJA](https://www.usar.army.mil/Portals/98/Documents/OSJA/Advancing%20Advocacy.pdf)
- Trial advocacy courses commonly run a weekly critique of simulated exercises: identify the misstep, explain why, show the better method. Some use peer critique, where students not performing critique each advocate. — [Univ. of Baltimore syllabus](http://www.law.ubalt.edu/faculty/sylfall17/LAW%20825.422%20Clarke%20Fall%202017.pdf); [mock trial assessment paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249019282_The_Unique_Challenges_Posed_by_Mock_Trial_Evaluation_and_Assessment_of_a_Simulation_Course)
- A 2025 qualitative study (India) examined evaluation rubrics combined with self-assessment in trial simulations, using observation and interviews with teaching judges and trainees. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391742568_Transforming_Legal_Education_in_India_The_Role_of_Clinical_Legal_Education_and_Trial_Advocacy)

### Inferences: proposed LexSim assessment design (my synthesis, not a sourced standard)
- **Scenario design.** Each scenario seeds 1-3 tagged pressure tactics, each linked to a row of the rules table. Some are **legitimate** (the student should answer them on the merits): a burden-of-proof challenge, a costs-risk argument, a settlement puff. Others are **illegitimate** (the student should spot them and use the procedural remedy): a misquoted article, a repeat recusal, a personal attack, a fake competing offer.
  - Expected remedies: ask the judge to warn under Art. 167(4); object in correct form per p.56; correct the record.
  - Escalate the pressure across levels.
- **Three-level outcome per tactic:**
  - **Avoided:** the student recognises the tactic, names it or uses the proper remedy, and stays on their own argument without being drawn in.
  - **Recovered:** the student was initially affected but corrected within N turns. Signs of being affected: conceding a point, answering off-topic, matching hostility, accepting a false fact. Correcting means re-asserting the point, objecting, or asking the court to correct the record.
  - **Fell into the trap:** the effect was not corrected before the phase ended. Examples: conceded a false fact, abandoned a valid claim under a puffed threat, responded with insult (which is itself a breach of p.11 / Art. 9(1)(7)), or accepted a fabricated authority.
- **Rubric dimensions, scored 0-3 each:**
  - recognition (identifies the tactic);
  - legal response (correct article or remedy cited);
  - composure and correct form (p.55-56, Art. 167 decorum: "Hurmatli sud");
  - staying on theory of the case;
  - accuracy (did not adopt false facts).
- **Debrief.** Use a Hampel-style structure, one point per exercise:
  - Headline: the single most important tactic missed.
  - Playback: verbatim quote of the transcript turn.
  - Reason: why it mattered, citing the rule.
  - Remedy: what to say.
  - Demonstration: a model answer.
  - Replay: rerun the same moment.

- Primary ICCA and NITA method documents were not text-readable or accessible. The step descriptions come from secondary teaching materials.
- No Uzbek law-school moot or advocacy-training rubric was found.
