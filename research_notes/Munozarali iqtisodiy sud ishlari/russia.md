# Landmark contested B2B cases from Russian arbitrazh courts (VAS RF Presidium / SKES VS RF) for Uzbek moot-court scenarios

Scope note: I used about 17 tool calls. Six cases are confirmed with a case number and a source. Two of them (Cases 1 and 3) have full instance-by-instance facts. Several Garant pages came back with garbled encoding, so some facts and amounts are only partly confirmed; this is flagged inline. Topics I could not verify inside this budget are listed under Gaps. They are independent guarantees, unjust enrichment, carriage and lost cargo, and supply of defective goods. Do not build scenarios on them without further checking.

Uzbek equivalents: RF Art. 333 is roughly Uzbek CC Art. 326 (given in the brief). I did not verify any other Uzbek article numbers in this session. The report writer should check them against the current Uzbek Civil Code (lex.uz) before citing them.

## Which VAS/SKES decisions are turning points, and what were the facts and holdings?

### Takeaway
On penalty reduction (Art. 333), the line runs from VAS 11680/10 (2011) through Plenum No. 81 (2011) and VAS 4231/14 (2014). It moved from "the refinancing rate is the floor, and the debtor must ask for reduction and prove disproportionality" to "going below the rate is not automatically an error". On leases, SKES 310-ЭС19-26908 (2020) held that a missing handover act is not conclusive proof that the tenant still uses the premises. On construction and services acts (Art. 753), SKES 305-ЭС19-9109 (2019) and 305-ЭС25-5342 (2025) form a contrasting pair. On COVID, SKES 305-ЭС21-12558 (2021) refused to treat the pandemic as grounds to terminate a long-term lease.

### Cited Findings

**CASE 1: Penalty reduction requires the debtor's application and proof (Art. 333 RF CC, roughly UZ CC Art. 326)**
- Presidium VAS RF Resolution of 13.01.2011 No. 11680/10, case No. А41-13284/09, published in Vestnik VAS 2011 No. 4 — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/1699913/)
- Holding: the Presidium quashed the appeal and cassation rulings that had reduced the penalty and reinstated the first-instance decision. The respondent had neither applied for a reduction nor produced evidence that the penalty was disproportionate — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/1699913/)
- After the lower courts' reduction, the penalty fell below 1% per annum. The penalty was calculated under Part 9 of Art. 9 of the Public Procurement Law (94-FZ of 21.07.2005), so this is a public-procurement contract between legal entities — [WebSearch summary of Garant/commentary](https://www.garant.ru/article/524832/)
- The Presidium named the Central Bank refinancing rate as the proportionality benchmark: reducing below it is possible only in exceptional cases. The debtor's financial difficulties are not a ground for reduction — [Garant commentary](https://www.garant.ru/article/524832/); [Vegas Lex](https://www.vegaslex.ru/mobile/analytics/publications/79962/)
- Numbers legible in the (garbled) Garant text: agreements of 25.08.2008 No. 7/55 and 23.03.2009 No. 9/3; amounts of 2,850,000 and 3,000,000 rubles; a principal of 257,895 rubles; sums of 39,700 and 27,839.56 rubles; a period of 10.12.2008 to 20.08.2009 (253 days); and a rate of 1/300 of the refinancing rate. UNCERTAIN: which sum is the debt and which is the penalty could not be read. Verify against the clean text before using the figures — [base.garant.ru](https://base.garant.ru/1799913/)

**CASE 1a: Follow-up. Reducing below a single CB rate is not automatically an error**
- Presidium VAS RF Resolution of 01.07.2014 No. 4231/14, case No. А40-41623/2013. The applicant was ООО «ТЗК АЭРОФЬЮЭЛЗ»; the dispute was a penalty under a supply contract. The first and appeal instances had reduced the penalty substantially, and the FAS of the Moscow District (cassation, 10.02.2014) changed this. The Presidium quashed the cassation ruling — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/70609304/)
- Holding: a reduction below a single CB rate by the first or appeal court does not by itself show a misapplication of Art. 333 and does not oblige cassation to award more. The CB rate is applied to the amount of the overdue obligation — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/70609304/)
- CONFLICT: secondary sources give different figures for the reduced penalty, 293,148 rubles (cited to zakon.ru) and 100,000 rubles (uslugijurista.ru). The role of ТЗК (plaintiff or defendant) is also unclear. Check the full text — [Zakon.ru review](https://zakon.ru/blog/2022/5/24/zavetnye_tri_trojki_obzor_sudebnoj_praktiki_po_snizheniyu_neustojki_po_st_333_gk)
- Background norm: Plenum VAS No. 81 of 22.12.2011 puts the burden of proving disproportionality on the debtor, and the court must give reasons for any reduction — [summary](https://vernoe18.ru/novosti/postanovlenie-plenuma-vas-rf-ot-22122011-n-81)

**CASE 2: Lease. No return act does not mean the lease continues (Art. 621(2), Art. 622, Art. 655 RF CC)**
- SKES VS RF Determination of 15.05.2020 No. 310-ЭС19-26908, case No. А35-6435/2018 (Arbitrazh Court of Kursk Region) — [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/)
- Parties: the landlord was an individual entrepreneur (ИП Рыльская Г.); the tenant was ООО «Курсклифтстрой» — [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/)
- Facts:
  - In January 2015 a metal hangar was leased until May 2015 at 113,000 rubles per month.
  - The contract required one month's written notice before vacating and return of the hangar under a return act.
  - No transfer act was signed at the start either.
  - In June 2015 the tenant paid rent for the whole term.
  - In May 2018 the landlord demanded about 3.9 million rubles for June 2015 to June 2018, arguing that the lease had renewed for an indefinite term because no return act was signed.
  - Source: [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/)
- Instances — [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/):
  - First instance and appeal: the claim was dismissed. The tenant used the hangar only during the term, paid in full, and stopped using it afterwards.
  - Cassation (district court): reversed and remanded. Vacating without terminating the lease and without a return act does not release the tenant from rent.
  - SKES: quashed the cassation ruling and reinstated the dismissal. A missing notice and return act "может свидетельствовать, но не является безусловным доказательством" ("may indicate, but is not conclusive proof") of continued use. Art. 621(2) turns on actual continued use, not on paperwork. The cassation court had re-weighed the evidence, exceeding its powers under Art. 286 APC. Point 13 of VAS Information Letter No. 66 (11.01.2002) did not apply. Because no transfer act was signed at the start, the missing return act was not decisive.
- Related rules:
  - VAS Information Letter No. 66 of 11.01.2002: rent is not recoverable if the landlord handed over the property without the documents needed to operate it. Rent for the period after expiry until a return act is signed is recoverable at the contract rate — [Klerk (text of Letter 66)](https://www.klerk.ru/doc/562403/); [arbitr.ru](https://arbitr.ru/materials/2986?path=%2Farxiv%2Fvas_info_letter%2F)
  - Presidium VAS of 13.09.2011 No. 4381/11 (case А40-168376/09-89-1159) is cited by the Volgograd court's methodological recommendations for the view that actual use can prove transfer without an act. FACTS NOT VERIFIED — [Volgograd arbitr.ru](https://volgograd.arbitr.ru/node/21113)
  - Those recommendations also say that a tenant who uses premises without obstruction but relies on the missing act can be denied protection as abusing rights (Art. 10) — [Volgograd arbitr.ru](https://volgograd.arbitr.ru/node/21113)

**CASE 3: One-sided act without delivering the result. The contractor loses (Arts. 702, 711, 720, 753, 328(3) RF CC)**
- SKES VS RF Determination of 03.10.2025 No. 305-ЭС25-5342, case No. А40-70779/2024. The plaintiff-contractor was ООО «Р-ДИЗАЙН»; the defendant-customer was ООО «Торговый дом Промышленность» — [EG-online](https://www.eg-online.ru/article/500058/)
- Facts — [EG-online](https://www.eg-online.ru/article/500058/):
  - In September 2023 the parties signed a framework contract and a supplementary agreement for producing a video.
  - The contractor could move the deadline by the length of any delay in the advance payment. The work deadline was 03.11.2023.
  - The customer never paid the advance. The contractor still made a 1 min 17 sec video and says it showed it on 16.11.2023.
  - An act dated 16.11.2023 was sent on 01.12.2023 and received on 22.12.2023. It was not signed and carried no link to the video.
  - The contract expired on 31.12.2023.
  - The file link was first given at the hearing on 10.06.2024.
  - Claims: the price, a penalty on the advance and a penalty on the final payment. The rouble amounts are not in the source.
  - Clause 6.5: the customer must sign the act or send a reasoned refusal within 5 working days.
- Instances — [EG-online](https://www.eg-online.ru/article/500058/):
  - First instance: dismissed. With no advance paid, the contractor was not obliged to start work. The result was delivered months late, and the customer had lost interest.
  - Appeal and cassation: granted the claim, because there was no reasoned refusal and the customer had not rebutted the act.
  - SKES: quashed both and reinstated the first-instance decision. The contract required delivering a link to the result together with the act and invoice. Without the result, volume and quality could not be checked. The lack of a reasoned refusal alone cannot make a one-sided act the basis for payment. Art. 328(3): a party cannot demand counter-performance without having performed its own obligation.

**CASE 4: COVID-19 is not a ground to terminate a long-term lease (Arts. 451, 401, 611, 614 RF CC; Law 98-FZ of 01.04.2020, Art. 19)**
- SKES VS RF Determination of 23.12.2021 No. 305-ЭС21-12558, case No. А40-100692/2020 — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/)
- Holding: the SKES quashed the cassation ruling and reinstated the appeal ruling that refused to terminate a non-residential lease. Terminating a long-term lease would have placed the negative effects of the restrictions on the landlord alone. A tenant is released from rent only where it could not use the property for reasons beyond its control — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/)
- Partly legible numbers — [Garant (garbled)](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/):
  - lease of 20.05.2014 No. 1/2014; area of 2,274.1 sq. m; term to 31.12.2022;
  - Art. 401, 451, 611 and 614 CC and Arts. 65, 68 and 71 APC are cited;
  - the figures 12.6% and 24% appear (probably revenue decline, UNCERTAIN), as does a reference to X5 Retail Group (role UNCERTAIN; possibly the tenant group).
- Fragments indicate a rent-arrears claim for October 2020 to January 2021, in which the court found no ground to release the tenant from rent or to reduce the penalty. UNCERTAIN, check the full text — [search summary of Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/)
- Zakon.ru commentary: the SKES found that, on these facts, the pandemic was not a material change of circumstances — [Zakon.ru](https://zakon.ru/blog/2022/01/24/covid-19_doshel_do_verhovnogo_suda_rf__kommentarij_k_opredeleniyu_sudebnoj_kollegii_po_ekonomicheski)
- Bridge Group: termination of a real-estate lease is not allowed even in a pandemic without proof of a legal right to withdraw unilaterally. The tenant's request to reduce the rent had been refused, which started the dispute — [Bridge Group](https://bridge-group.ru/articles/yuridicheskiy-servis/rastorzhenie-dogovora-kommercheskoy-arendy-v-usloviyakh-pandemii-razbor-sudebnogo-keysa-/)
- Background: VS RF Review No. 1 of 21.04.2020 (Q7) says force majeure must be assessed against the specific debtor and situation. It does not end an obligation that can still be performed once the circumstances pass — [Kontur](https://kontur.ru/articles/5770)
- CONFLICT: sources disagree on whether a lack of funds caused by the pandemic can count as force majeure under Review No. 1 — [glavkniga](https://glavkniga.ru/situations/s511364); [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/legal/120601-koronavirus-i-fors-mazhor-poziciya-verhovnogo-suda)

**CASE 6: Termination damages, current price and substitute transaction (Art. 393.1 RF CC; Plenum VS No. 7 of 24.03.2016, paras 12–14)**
- SKES VS RF Determination of 23.05.2025 No. 305-ЭС25-619, case No. А40-299210/2023, a supply contract. The SKES quashed the lower acts and remanded, because the courts had not established the periods of delay. The substitute purchase may be made before or after termination of the supply contract. ### Inferences
- For scenario design, Cases 2 and 3 are the strongest. The facts are complete, there are three-tier disagreements, and the outcome turns on evidence (actual use or vacating; delivery of the result). Case 1 is a strong Art. 326 UZ scenario: a student must file the reduction request and evidence at first instance, or lose it.
- Teaching points by case:
  - Case 1. Debtor's counsel must apply for reduction at first instance and bring evidence (market rates, the creditor's lack of losses, the CB rate). Creditor's counsel argues the burden of proof (Plenum 81) and the rate floor. Common mistake: asking for reduction for the first time on appeal, or relying only on financial hardship.
  - Case 2. Landlord's counsel relies on the missing return act and on Letter No. 66 p. 13. Tenant's counsel proves vacating (payments stopping, utility records, the landlord's 3-year silence, no act at the start either). Common mistake: treating paperwork as substance.
  - Case 3. Contractor's counsel must prove the result was actually delivered in the contract's form. An unsigned act without the result fails. Customer's counsel uses Art. 328(3) and lost interest (Art. 708 and 405(2), my inference, not cited by the source).
  - Case 4. Tenant's counsel must show, specifically, impossibility of use or all four conditions of Art. 451(2). Landlord's counsel argues that the risk is shared and that 98-FZ provides the remedy, which is deferral or reduction, not termination.
- On Uzbek transferability, the following are inferred and not verified. Uzbek CC articles on penalty reduction (326), change of circumstances, reciprocal performance, and contractor acceptance likely mirror the RF ones. Article numbers must be checked on lex.uz.

## Where did lower courts and the highest court disagree, and why?

### Takeaway
In every verified case the top court reversed the court below it. The usual pattern is that the cassation or appeal court applied a formal rule (no act, no reasoned refusal, the rate floor) or re-weighed evidence. The highest court then restored the fact-based first-instance (or appeal) decision.

### Cited Findings
- 11680/10: the appeal and cassation courts reduced the penalty on their own initiative, to below 1% per annum. The Presidium restored the first-instance decision, because the debtor had made no application and produced no proof — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/1699913/)
- 4231/14: the cassation court increased the penalty because it was below the CB rate. The Presidium restored the lower courts' reduction, holding that the rate is not an automatic floor for cassation — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/70609304/)
- 310-ЭС19-26908: the cassation court held that the formal absence of a return act creates a rent obligation. The SKES restored the first-instance and appeal fact-finding, holding that cassation exceeded its powers under Art. 286 APC — [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/)
- 305-ЭС25-5342: the appeal and cassation courts held that no reasoned refusal means payment is due. The SKES restored the first instance, because the result was never delivered — [EG-online](https://www.eg-online.ru/article/500058/)
- 305-ЭС21-12558: the cassation court terminated the lease (pandemic). The SKES restored the appeal ruling refusing termination — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/)

### Inferences
- Russian moot scenarios can be framed as "formalism vs substance". The losing side at the top level usually relied on a document-based presumption that the other side rebutted with evidence of actual conduct.
- Contrasting 305-ЭС19-9109 (contractor wins) with 305-ЭС25-5342 (contractor loses) gives a ready-made pair of debate positions on Art. 753(4).

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether the 2011 refinancing-rate approach survived the 2015 amendments to Art. 333 and Plenum No. 7 (2016) paras 69–81. Plenum No. 7 is widely understood to have replaced Plenum No. 81 in large part, but this was not checked in this session.

## Which arguments decided the outcome?

### Takeaway
The deciding factor was evidence of actual conduct: use or vacating of the premises, actual delivery of the work result, and whether the debtor applied for and proved disproportionality. Formal documents (acts, the absence of a refusal) were treated as rebuttable evidence, not conclusive proof.

### Cited Findings
- Burden and timing: no application and no evidence of disproportionality from the debtor meant the penalty could not be reduced — [Garant 11680/10](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/1699913/); the burden is on the debtor under Plenum 81 — [summary](https://vernoe18.ru/novosti/postanovlenie-plenuma-vas-rf-ot-22122011-n-81)
- Conduct of the parties in the lease case: full rent payment for the term, use ending after expiry, the landlord's 3-year delay, and no act at the start — [Advokatskaya Gazeta](https://www.advgazeta.ru/novosti/vs-zashchitil-arendatora-ne-podpisavshego-akt-o-vozvrate-imushchestva-ego-vladeltsu-posle-okonchaniya-arendy/)
- Delivery of the result in the contractual form (a link with the act and invoice) and timing (after the contract expired); unsupported claims of an earlier demonstration were rejected — [EG-online](https://www.eg-online.ru/article/500058/)
- COVID: a specific showing of impossibility of use is required; general pandemic effects are not enough — [Garant](https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/403217460/); [Kontur on Review No. 1](https://kontur.ru/articles/5770)

### Inferences
- Students should be trained to build an evidence matrix for each side: documents, correspondence, payments and timeline. The highest courts decided on that matrix, not on a single missing signature.
