# Contested B2B disputes in Uzbek economic courts and the Plenum resolutions that govern them (material for courtroom training scenarios)

Notes on the sources. The most detailed primary material is the set of Higher Economic Court (Oliy xo'jalik sudi, VHS) and Supreme Court (Oliy sud) cassation rulings that lex.uz publishes as extracts ("[Извлечение]"), each with a headnote. All the full-text cases below came from lex.uz pages I downloaded and read. Most public, fully reasoned B2B rulings on lex.uz date from 2009–2016. In the time available I found very few 2020+ contractual B2B rulings with full text: the two 2023 Supreme Court rulings I found are corporate disputes. Party names are given exactly as published. Some 2023 rulings anonymise them (for example "M H (C) L").

Terminology: before 2017 the courts were "хозяйственные суды" (khozyaistvennye), governed by the old Хозяйственный процессуальный кодекс (ХПК). Today they are economic courts (iqtisodiy sudlar), governed by the Economic Procedure Code (ЭПК/IPK) of 24.01.2018, and the Higher Economic Court was merged into the Supreme Court. Article numbers from the old ХПК that appear in older cases are marked as such.

## Q1. Which Plenum resolutions govern penalties (FK 326), lease, supply, construction contracts and acceptance acts, unjust enrichment, suretyship and carriage, and what concrete rules do they contain?

### Takeaway
The core set is all still in force, though amended by Supreme Court Plenums in 2018–2025:
- VHS Plenum No. 163 of 15.06.2007: liability, penalties, FK 326 and 327.
- VHS Plenum No. 103 of 04.03.2002: the Law "On the contractual legal basis of business entities' activity".
- VHS Plenum No. 234 of 01.12.2011: lease.
- VHS Plenum No. 306 of 23.12.2016: construction contracts and acceptance acts.
- Joint SC/VHS Plenum No. 13/150 of 22.12.2006: security for credit obligations, including suretyship.

These resolutions contain very specific, citable rules, which suit "argue the rule" training exercises. I found no dedicated Plenum resolution on carriage or on unjust enrichment as such. Unjust enrichment (FK 1023) appears inside the lease and construction resolutions.

### Cited Findings

**Plenum VHS No. 163 of 15.06.2007, "On some issues of applying civil legislation on property liability for non-performance or improper performance of obligations"** — [lex.uz/docs/1636812](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.2:** a penalty (fine or penya) is a way of securing performance. Courts must examine whether it was calculated lawfully, whether it is justified and whether it is proportionate, and must set a "fair amount" (справедливый размер) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.3:** if the contract provides both a fine and a penya for the same breach, the claimant may demand only one form, unless legislation provides otherwise — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.3¹ (added by SC Plenum No. 39 of 16.12.2024):** if the contract is found unconcluded or invalid, any claim for liability measures under it is refused — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.3² (added by SC Plenum No. 39 of 16.12.2024):**
  - A public-procurement contract concluded outside the mandatory e-shop, tender or single-supplier channels is void under FK 116.
  - Amending a tendered contract by agreement of the parties, without a tender-commission protocol, is void.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.4 (the key FK 326 rule):**
  - The court may reduce a penalty, taking into account the degree of performance by the debtor, the parties' property (financial) position and the creditor's interests.
  - The reduced penalty may not fall below the interest under FK 327.
  - The parties' agreement to raise the statutory penalty (FK 263, part 2) is "not in itself a ground" for reducing it.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **pp.5–11 (FK 327 interest):**
  - FK 327 interest is liability for a monetary obligation.
  - A 360/30-day year is used.
  - Interest accrues until actual payment.
  - The court may choose the bank rate on the day the claim is filed or on the day of judgment, whichever is closer to the rates over the period of delay.
  - No interest is charged on interest.
  - FK 327 interest differs from loan or credit interest (FK 734, 744, 748).
  - **p.11:** FK 327 interest may itself be reduced, by analogy with FK 326 and under FK 335, or refused entirely.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.12 (fault, FK 333):** a debtor's lack of money, its counterparties' breaches or the market absence of goods do NOT release it from FK 327 interest or from a penalty — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.13:** for monetary obligations, the creditor may claim EITHER FK 327 interest OR a contractual or statutory penya (one of these measures), without proving losses — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.15:** delivering goods without the (then-required) minimum 15% prepayment is no ground to refuse a penalty or interest — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.16:** penya, fines and interest run from the day after the performance deadline. If no deadline was set, they run after 7 days from the creditor's demand — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.17:** if the creditor is in delay (for example, it failed to give its account details), no interest is owed for that period (FK 338, part 2) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.19:** losses must be proved by a reasoned calculation and evidence. Losses are recovered only in the part not covered by the penalty (wording of SC Plenum No. 27 of 24.12.2019) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **p.20:** Plenum No. 163 repealed VHS Plenum No. 77 of 05.02.1999. Under the old No. 77 (p.4), a penalty not exceeding the principal could be recovered without examining losses. A penalty above the principal could be reduced "down to the principal debt". This older "cap at principal" practice rule is no longer in force but is still cited in commentary — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812); old rule described in [buxgalter.uz commentary](https://buxgalter.uz/doc?id=397609_po_sporu_o_vzyskanii_shtrafnyh_sankciy_za_narushenie_usloviy_dogovora_postavki&prodid=1_vse_zakonodatelstvo_uzbekistana)

**Plenum VHS No. 103 of 04.03.2002 (on the Law "On the contractual legal basis of business entities' activity" No. 670-I of 29.08.1998)** — [lex.uz/docs/1643250](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.2:** acceptance must be full and unconditional. Acceptance "on other terms" is a counter-offer. Performance (shipment, payment), even partial, within the acceptance period counts as acceptance — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.3:** failure to use written form does not invalidate a business contract. It bars witness testimony, but invoices, waybills, powers of attorney, acts and correspondence may be used (FK 108, FK 363 on interpretation) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.5:** if essential terms are missing and cannot be determined, the contract is unconcluded and non-contractual rules apply — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.6:** if there is no price, the average market price applies (FK 356) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.7:** an obligation with no deadline must be performed within 7 days of demand (FK 242) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.10:** contractual liability prevails. For breaches the contract does not cover, the statutory penalties of Law 670-I, Chapter 5 (arts. 25–33) apply — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.11:** liability requires fault (FK 333). The statutory penalty (FK 263) and losses (FK 324) may be recovered. A penalty is "primarily aimed at compensating damage", so courts should determine the damage and may order an expert examination with the parties' consent — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.12 (art. 32 of the Law):**
  - "Evasion of payment" means non-payment when payment was possible, groundless non-recognition of the debt, refusal to sign a reconciliation act, or fictitious insolvency.
  - A debtor who acknowledges the debt and takes steps to pay is NOT evading.
  - Where evasion is proven, a fine of 15% of the evaded sum may be imposed (wording of VHS Plenum No. 307 of 23.12.2016).
  - Penya is calculated on the unperformed part, not on the contract price.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)
- **p.13:** courts must check the size and period of the sanctions and their legal or contractual basis, recalculate where needed and may appoint an expert — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1643250)

**Statutory penalty rates (Law No. 670-I)** — [lex.uz/docs/18942](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/18942)
- **Art. 25 (late delivery, short delivery, works not done):** 0.5% of the unperformed part per day of delay. The total may not exceed 50% of the price of the undelivered goods, unperformed works or services.
- **Art. 32, part 2 (late payment for goods, works or services; wording of Law ZRU-372 of 14.05.2014):** 0.4% of the overdue sum per day, but no more than 50% of the overdue sum.
- **Art. 32, part 1:** a fine for evasion of payment. Plenum No. 103 p.12 sets it at 15%.
- Other fines:
  - art. 28 (no marking or packaging): 5%;
  - art. 29 (letter of credit not used): 5%;
  - art. 30 (late shipping documents): 1%;
  - art. 31 (refusal to take goods): 5%, or 10% for perishables;
  - art. 33 (unjustified debit without acceptance): 10%.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/18942)

**Plenum VHS No. 234 of 01.12.2011, "On some issues of economic courts' application of civil legislation on property lease"** (amended by SC Plenum No. 17 of 19.05.2018, No. 33 of 20.11.2023 and No. 10 of 29.04.2025) — [lex.uz/docs/2004727](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.4:**
  - A lease involving a legal entity must be in writing (FK 539).
  - State registration of a lease of immovable property is mandatory, except leases of buildings for less than one year. An unregistered lease is invalid (FK 112, with the consequences under FK 114).
  - A party may sue to compel registration.
  - Amendments to a registrable lease must also be registered (FK 384).
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.5:** the court must demand the lessor's title documents. State property is leased only through the territorial departments of the Centre under the State Assets Management Agency (CM Resolution No. 660 of 14.12.2023, wording of 2025) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.7:** if the property is handed over without the documents needed to use it, the lessor may not demand rent (FK 541, part 3) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.9 (key rules on unsigned transfer acts):**
  - The duty to pay rent arises only from transfer of the property. A lessor who has not transferred it may not demand rent for that period.
  - A tenant who stays on after expiry pays for the whole period of use.
  - **Added 2018:** the absence of a transfer act (FK 541, part 2) is NOT a ground to refuse the lessor's rent claim, if entry into the relationship and the tenant's acceptance of the property are proven by written or other evidence.
  - **Wording of 2023:** if the rent and the method of fixing it are not stated, the lease is unconcluded. If property was nevertheless transferred, the lessor may claim its return and payment for use under **FK 1023 (unjust enrichment)**.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.10:** if the lease is invalid, the tenant reimburses the value of use. That value is measured by the contract rent or, failing that, under FK 356 — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.11.1:** early termination at the lessor's request (FK 551) requires a prior written warning to the tenant. Failing to give it counts as non-compliance with the pretrial procedure. FK 552 (termination at the tenant's request) requires no such warning, but FK 384, part 2 applies — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
- **p.12:** a lease is renewed for an indefinite term (FK 553, part 4) if the tenant keeps using the property after expiry AND the lessor does not object — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)

**Plenum VHS No. 306 of 23.12.2016, "On some issues of economic courts' application of legislation in disputes arising from construction contracts"** (amended 2018, 2020 and 2025) — [lex.uz/docs/3106546](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.2.1–2.3:**
  - If essential terms are missing, the construction contract is unconcluded. That is stated only in the reasoning of the judgment; a claim to "declare the contract unconcluded" is refused or the proceedings terminated (EPC 154(1)(1) and 110(1)).
  - The contractor can still recover the value of works it proves were performed, but NOT a penalty, interest or losses.
  - **Since 2025:** this rule does not protect contracts concluded in breach of public-procurement procedures.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.4:** a contract that had to be tendered but was concluded without a tender is void (FK 116). Changes agreed by the parties outside the tender commission's protocol are void — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.6 (additional works, FK 670 and 671):**
  - Additional works costing ≤10% of the estimate: the customer may amend the design and estimate documents itself.
  - Above 10%: a separate agreement is required.
  - If the customer does not reply within 10 days, the contractor must suspend work, and downtime losses fall on the customer.
  - A contractor that did not notify the customer and did not suspend loses the right to payment for the additional works (FK 670, part 6), unless the work was urgent to save the object; the burden of proof is on the contractor.
  - The customer's acceptance of unnotified additional works does NOT mean it agrees to pay for them.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.7.1:** a customer that terminates for slow work (FK 642, part 2) must prove it lost interest in late performance. Otherwise its claim for losses is refused — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.9 (acceptance acts, FK 646 and 680):**
  - The acceptance act is signed by both parties. If one party refuses, this is noted on the act and the other party signs it alone.
  - A **unilateral act** may be held invalid only if the court finds the reasons for refusal justified. Justified reasons are limited to:
    - defects that rule out use of the result and cannot be remedied;
    - the contractor's evasion of preliminary tests.
  - Invalidity of a unilateral act is stated only in the reasoning; a separate claim to declare it invalid is refused or terminated.
  - A claim to compel the other party to sign the act is refused, because the unilateral act already serves as evidence.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.9.1–9.2:**
  - The customer may rely only on defects recorded in the act, except hidden defects.
  - Hidden defects must be notified within 10 days of discovery (FK 646, part 4).
  - A quality dispute obliges the court to appoint an expert examination at a party's request.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.10:** there is no mandatory expert examination for disputes about the volume of works. If the act was signed, an expert examination is ordered only if the customer brings evidence. If there is no signed act and the volume is disputed, the court must order one at a party's request — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **p.11:** an act signed by a customer's representative without authority is not acceptance. It may still be one piece of evidence if the customer's refusal to sign was unjustified — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **pp.13–15:**
  - The default guarantee period is 10 years (FK 682).
  - If the contract defers part of the payment until after the guarantee period, the contractor cannot claim that part before then.
  - Contractor's savings: under FK 637 the contractor keeps the contract price unless the customer proves the savings affected quality.
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)

- **Appeal and cassation deadlines (EPC):**
  - appeal: 1 month — [gov.uz](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/611/document/1559);
  - cassation: 6 months from the date the first-instance decision enters into force (Uzbek EPC text) — [lex.uz EPC](https://lex.uz/mact/-3523891). The gov.uz summary says 1 month for cassation. The two sources conflict, so check the current EPC article before relying on either.

**Text of FK 326** (per lex.uz and the case texts):
- Part 1: if the penalty is clearly disproportionate to the consequences of the breach, the court may reduce it.
- Part 2: "in exceptional cases", considering the interests of both the debtor and the creditor, the court may reduce the penalty.
- Sources: [lex.uz/docs/2882730](https://lex.uz/docs/2882730); [lex.uz/docs/2831719](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2831719)

### Inferences
- For a simulator, the "rule bank" is very concrete and lends itself to scoring:
  - one form of penalty per breach (163 p.3);
  - penya OR FK 327 interest, not both (163 p.13);
  - floor at the FK 327 interest rate (163 p.4);
  - reduction factors: degree of performance, financial position, creditor's interest (163 p.4);
  - the 50% statutory caps (Law 670-I, arts. 25 and 32);
  - a unilateral acceptance act stands unless the refusal was justified (306 p.9);
  - an unsigned lease transfer act can be cured by other evidence (234 p.9).
- The Supreme Court Plenums of 2024–2025 have tightened public-procurement voidness. This creates a modern "trap": a party claiming a penalty under a tender contract amended outside the tender commission loses its penalty claim.

### Gaps
- I found no dedicated Plenum resolution on carriage (перевозка), on unjust enrichment as a standalone topic, or on suretyship outside credit agreements. A Plenum on supply contracts may exist, but I did not find or verify one.
- I did not confirm the current article numbers of the FK on the limitation period (I believe FK 150, a 3-year general period, but did not verify this) or of the EPC on the pretrial claim requirement. These need checking on lex.uz.
- I did not verify the full text of Plenum 13/150 or SC Plenum 10/2024.

## Q2. Which specific decided economic court cases are publicly reported in enough detail? (case files usable as scenarios)

### Takeaway
I found six detailed B2B contractual cases with case numbers, amounts and contract clauses (A–F below), plus two 2023 Supreme Court corporate cases.
- One is from 2016, two from 2013, one from 2015. The 2003 supply case and the 2009/2010 cases are pre-2015.
- All are VHS cassation rulings or a published case commentary, sourced from lex.uz and buxgalter.uz.
- The two 2023 Supreme Court rulings are corporate disputes (exclusion of a participant; void simple-partnership contract), not contract claims.

### Cited Findings

#### Case A — Penya vs FK 327 interest and an unreasoned reduction of penya (2016, current-law logic). Machinery lease with crew.
Ruling of the VHS Judicial Collegium No. 20-1606/2095 of 03.10.2016 — [lex.uz/docs/3079484 (Uzbek)](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3079484); Russian headnote at [lex.uz/docs/3079487](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3079487)

- **Parties:**
  - claimant: "GOLDEN MILLENNIUM TECHNOLOGY" MChJ (lessor);
  - defendant: "ENTER Engineering Pte.Ltd" (lessee). The claim was first filed against its permanent establishment in Qorako'l district; the company itself was joined as defendant by ruling of 28.03.2016, and the proceedings against the permanent establishment were terminated.
- **Contract:** No. EE-GMT/4 of 03.01.2015, lease of machinery with crew.
  - Clause 1.1: services of 616,660,216 so'm provided from January to 30.06.2015; 358,333,871 so'm paid.
  - Clause 3.5: payment within 15 banking days of the invoice.
  - Clause 4.1: liability under the Law on contractual legal basis.
  - Clause 4.2: penya of 0.01% per day, capped at 20% of the overdue sum.
- **Claim:** 258,326,345 so'm principal + 13,140,718 so'm penya + 32,205,980 so'm bank interest (FK 327).
- **1st instance (05.04.2016):** awarded the principal and bank interest in full, but reduced penya to 7,884,430 so'm. **Appeal (09.06.2016):** upheld.
- **Cassation (03.10.2016), defendant's complaint partly granted:**
  - The reduction of penya was premature. The penya was calculated correctly and was proportionate. There was **no document on the debtor's financial position** in the file, and the acts showed no complaint about service quality. Penya was **raised back to the full 13,140,718 so'm**.
  - The bank interest of 32,205,980 so'm was **cancelled**. The contract (cl. 4.1–4.2) provided only for penya, and under Plenum 163 p.13 the creditor may claim only one of the two measures.
  - The state fee was recalculated from 1,518,365 to 1,357,335 so'm.
  - Norms cited: FK 326; Plenum 163 pp.2 and 13; Plenum 161 (judgment) p.1; old ХПК arts. 186 and 188.
- **Teaching points:**
  - A debtor seeking reduction must PROVE its financial position and disproportionality. A judge cannot reduce on its own initiative without evidence.
  - A creditor that stacks penya + FK 327 interest loses one of them.
  - A debtor's appeal can backfire: the defendant appealed and ended up paying more penya.
  - Contract clauses 4.1 and 4.2 were decisive.

#### Case B — Penalty reduction "in exceptional cases"; fine vs penya for the same breach; subcontractor fault as defence (construction, 2013)
Ruling of the VHS Judicial Collegium No. 10-1312/3177 of 26.08.2013 — [lex.uz/docs/2882730](https://lex.uz/docs/2882730)

- **Parties:**
  - claimant: Unified Customer Service of the Ministry of Defence (a state customer, but the dispute is contractual);
  - defendant: "Ulkan qurilish servis" MChJ.
- **Contract:** No. 5 of 17.02.2010, awarded by tender. Construction of a 5-storey, 50-flat residential building in Chirchiq; price 2,701,344,590 so'm.
  - Additional agreement No. 5/1 of 30.04.2012 (on the basis of tender commission protocol No. 9/4-12 of 16.04.2012): deadline moved to 30.08.2012.
  - Additional agreement No. 5/2 of 26.11.2012 (protocol No. 16/4-12 of 05.11.2012): final price 2,699,680,629 so'm, completion by 01.12.2012.
  - Works left unperformed by 01.12.2012: 291,745,161 so'm.
- **Claim:** penalty of 134,786,264 so'm.
- **Decisions:**
  - 1st instance: 112,321,887 so'm penya. Because the contract provided different penalties for the same breach, the court applied only penya (Plenum 163 p.13, now p.3).
  - Appeal: **reduced penya to 56,000,000 so'm** under FK 326.
  - Cassation: upheld the appeal.
- **Contractor's argument:** it was not at fault. Third-party contractors engaged by the customer breached their own contracts, which made timely delivery impossible.
- **Norms cited:** FK 666, 673, 261, 326; Plenum 163 pp.2 and 13; Plenum 161 p.1.
- **Debatable:** whether the customer's other contractors' breaches excuse the contractor (FK 333, Plenum 163 p.12). Also, the ruling gives no explicit reasoning for halving the penalty.

#### Case C — Minimum floor of the reduced penalty, suretyship and novation (leasing, 2010)
Ruling of the VHS Judicial Collegium No. 10-1021/3457(17) of 12.07.2010 — [lex.uz/docs/2831719](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2831719)

- **Parties:**
  - claimant: OALK "O'ZAVIALIZING" (leasing company);
  - defendants: SP OAO "INTER TRANS SERVICE" (lessee) and OOO "LABZAK DAVR" (surety, jointly liable).
- **Contracts:**
  - Leasing contract No. 2006/2 of 14.12.2006 (20 GAZ-31105 cars).
  - Leasing contract No. 2007/2 of 16.03.2007 (60 gas-cylinder kits).
  - Suretyships of 13.12.2006 and 16.03.2007.
  - Transfer acts of 21.12.2006, 07.06.2007 and 31.07.2007.
  - Additional agreements No. 2 and No. 5 of 21.12.2009: the lessor cut accrued penya by 50%. The debtor acknowledged the debt and undertook to pay interest by 01.03.2010 and the penalty by 01.08.2010; it breached these terms too.
- **Claim:** 16,870,534.22 so'm interest + 30,294,760 so'm penya (the ruling also mentions 30,314,760).
- **1st instance (07.05.2010):** interest in full; penya **reduced to 4,000,000 so'm**, jointly against both defendants. The defendant argued that 10 of the 20 cars had been returned.
- **Claimant's argument:** the additional agreements amount to a novation (FK 347), so the penalty under them cannot be reduced.
- **Cassation:**
  - A novation does NOT bar reduction under FK 326.
  - However, 4,000,000 so'm was below the FK 327 interest floor (Plenum 163 p.4, para 2), so penya was **raised to 4,241,266 so'm**.
- **Teaching point:** a court that reduces a penalty must compute the FK 327 floor. A student must be able to do that calculation.

#### Case D — Supply in instalments; statutory penalty on top of contractual penalty; the buyer's defence that the supplier short-delivered (2001–2003; pre-2015 law, old Plenum 77)
Commentary "По спору о взыскании штрафных санкций за неисполнение договора поставки" (НТВ No. 27-28, 11.07.2003) — [buxgalter.uz](https://buxgalter.uz/doc?id=397609_po_sporu_o_vzyskanii_shtrafnyh_sankciy_za_narushenie_usloviy_dogovora_postavki&prodid=1_vse_zakonodatelstvo_uzbekistana). Parties anonymised; no case number published.

- **Parties:** a production cooperative (supplier) v a production association (buyer).
- **Contract:** one-year supply contract; delivery in 4 equal batches; payment 30 days after shipment; price in USD, payable in so'm at the Central Bank rate on the day of payment; contractual penya of 0.1% per day.
- **Claim:**
  - 142,964,465.56 so'm principal and 52,315,185 so'm penya, adding the statutory penya under Law 670-I, art. 32 (0.4% per day, up to 50%).
  - The buyer did not dispute a debt including exchange-rate difference of 71,543,126 so'm and had paid 54,939,287 so'm before the hearing. It pleaded a difficult financial position.
- **1st instance (Tashkent City Economic Court):** principal granted; only the contractual 0.1% penya awarded, i.e. 17,609,449 so'm. The statutory penya was refused as disproportionate (old Plenum 77 p.4).
- **Appeal:**
  - The buyer argued that a Ministry of Justice-registered accounting regulation (No. 1085, para 5) required the October 2001 exchange rate, which would give a difference of only 15,865,045.25 so'm. Rejected: the regulation applies only to prices set in foreign currency, whereas here the price was in so'm equivalent.
  - The supplier asked for a further 34,705,736 so'm of statutory penya. Also rejected.
- **Cassation (VHS):**
  - Set aside both instances and **refused the claim entirely**.
  - The supplier had itself short-delivered every batch, and the buyer had paid in full for what was actually delivered. So at the time of the first-instance hearing there was no debt.
- **Norms (commentary):** FK 440 part 2 (delivery schedule), 245 part 1 (currency clause), 236, 263, 326, 356; Law 670-I arts. 24–33; Plenum 103 pp.11 and 13; old Plenum 77 pp.3–4.
- **Teaching point:** before arguing about the penalty, check whether the principal debt exists at all (the parties' cross-performance). This is a good "hidden fact" scenario: the reconciliation of shipped versus paid quantities.

#### Case E — Suretyship expired before the claim; arbitration clause replaced by a later choice-of-court agreement (supply, 2015)
Ruling of the VHS Judicial Collegium No. 10-1416/21046 of 08.04.2015 — [lex.uz/docs/2839956](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/2839956)

- **Parties:**
  - claimant: OOO "Torgovyi dom 'Altai Shina'" (Dnipropetrovsk, Ukraine; supplier);
  - defendant: OOO "LOGOPROM BUSINESS" (Tashkent; buyer, the renamed OOO "PROMSTROYDOBICHA");
  - co-defendant: OOO "Mega-MEBIKO" (surety).
- **Contracts:**
  - Supply of tyres No. 174 of 19.05.2008: specifications of USD 316,100 and USD 268,832. Additional agreement of 26.05.2008 added cl. 9.3: penya of 12% per annum for late payment under cl. 3.3.
  - Supply No. 175 of 25.07.2008: USD 148,650 and USD 71,400.
  - Originally, disputes went to the ICAC at the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Ukraine. Separate agreements of 21.02.2013 moved disputes to the state court at the defendant's seat, under Uzbek law, with **"pretrial claim procedure not mandatory"**.
- **Debt:** USD 61,850 + USD 71,400 = USD 133,250, equal to **317,101,687.50 so'm** at 2,379 so'm/USD. Evidence: invoices, correspondence, reconciliation acts.
- **Suretyship contract No. 1 of 11.09.2012:**
  - Additional agreement No. 1 of 27.03.2013 extended it to debts shown in reconciliation acts.
  - Cl. 4.1: termination events.
  - Cl. 5.1: disputes go to the court at the surety's seat; **pretrial claim procedure mandatory**.
  - Cl. 6.2: **term until 11.09.2014**.
- **1st instance (16.01.2015):** refused the whole claim. The court treated the Ukrainian arbitration clause as governing and lifted the attachment of 317,101,687.50 so'm imposed on 21.11.2014.
- **Cassation (claimant's complaint + Tashkent prosecutor's protest, partly granted):**
  - Debt of 317,101,687.50 so'm recovered from LOGOPROM BUSINESS. The 2013 agreements are separate choice-of-court agreements, and FK 294 requires the debtor to be joined when the surety is sued.
  - **Surety released**: under FK 298, part 3 the suretyship ended on its contractual term, 11.09.2014, before the claim was filed.
  - Lifting the interim measure upheld (old ХПК art. 80).
  - Costs of 4,756,525.5 so'm state fee and 14,200 so'm postage awarded against the buyer.
- **Debatable points:**
  - Does "term of the suretyship contract" equal the suretyship term under FK 298?
  - Is the 2013 forum agreement valid against the arbitration clause?
  - The contrasting pretrial-claim clauses (not mandatory in the 2013 agreements, mandatory in the suretyship).

#### Case F — Services acceptance acts not signed by the customer; counterclaim not accepted (security services, 2012–2013)
Ruling of the VHS Judicial Collegium No. 10-1304/10764 of 28.10.2013 — [lex.uz/docs/2875644](https://lex.uz/docs/2875644)

- **Parties:**
  - claimant: OOO "Fort Leader" (security firm);
  - defendant: OOO "Green Team" (supermarket at Mukimi st. 1/1, Tashkent).
- **Contract:** No. 6 of 02.07.2012.
  - Cl. 4.1–4.2: 8,700,000 so'm per month, 100% prepayment.
  - Cl. 4.5: services are documented by an act signed by BOTH parties.
- **Course of events:**
  - August prepaid by payment order No. 138 of 31.07.2012; September not prepaid.
  - No acts were signed.
  - Customer's claim letter No. 24 of 13.08.2012; contractor's rejection No. 114 of 18.09.2012.
  - Contractor's letter No. 19 of 24.09.2012 proposed termination and signing of the September act.
- **Claim:** 16,497,600 so'm. **Counterclaim:** 8,700,000 so'm (return of the August prepayment) + 1,001,095.80 so'm interest + 1,000,000 so'm losses.
- **1st instance and appeal:**
  - Claim refused: no signed act means no proven service. 6,960,000 so'm for September refused.
  - Counterclaim partly granted: 8,700,000 so'm returned.
  - Interest refused: FK 420, parts 3–4 do not apply to services.
  - Losses refused: not proven.
- **Cassation (the contractor's complaint and the prosecutor's protest):**
  - Set aside and **remitted**.
  - The courts ignored the acts and letters that had been sent, including those received personally by the customer's representative I.Kh. Zhuraev.
  - They also ignored the logbooks of premises hand-over signed by both sides' staff, and failed to call those staff as witnesses.
  - The first instance had not formally accepted the counterclaim (old ХПК art. 120; Plenum 162 p.27).
  - Norms: FK 236, 703, 705; Plenum 161 p.3.
- **Note:** remittal is now generally barred under SC Plenum 10/2024, so today the cassation court would decide the case itself.
- **Teaching point:** a clause requiring a "bilateral act" versus other evidence of performance. This mirrors the construction rule in Plenum 306 p.9 and the lease rule in Plenum 234 p.9.

## Q3. Which issues are genuinely contested in Uzbek practice?

### Takeaway
The contested core is penalty reduction under FK 326:
- whether a reduction needs evidence (Case A says yes);
- how far a penalty can be cut (Case B halved it; the 2009 ruling cut it to 10%);
- the FK 327 interest floor (Case C);
- stacking penya with interest, or a fine with penya (Plenum 163 pp.3 and 13).

Close behind are acceptance-act disputes (unilateral or unsigned acts) and force majeure evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **How much do courts cut?**
  - A practitioner Q&A cited a practice of courts cutting a penya of 50m so'm on a 100m so'm debt (0.4% per day, cap 50%) down to 20m so'm.
  - A practising lawyer **rejected** the perception that courts "never award more than 20%": the amount is case-specific.
  — [gazeta.norma.uz](https://gazeta.norma.uz/publish/doc/text97048_summu_neustoyki_ustanovil_sud?paper=norma)
- **Effect of partial payment after filing:** a commentator advises not reducing the claim, because the days of delay are unchanged and the state fee is recovered in full even if the court cuts the penalty — [gazeta.norma.uz](https://gazeta.norma.uz/publish/doc/text101388_istec_imeet_pravo). This is consistent with SC Plenum 38/2024 on costs (snippet) — [nrm.uz](https://nrm.uz/contentf?doc=769358_postanovlenie_plenuma_verhovnogo_suda_respubliki_uzbekistan_ot_16_12_2024_g_n_38_o_vnesenii_izmeneniy_i_dopolneniy_v_postanovleniya_plenuma_verhovnogo_suda_respubliki_uzbekistan_o_praktike_vzyskaniya_sudebnyh_rashodov_po_ekonomicheskim_i_administrativnym_delam&products=1_vse_zakonodatelstvo_uzbekistana)
- **Reduction without evidence is reversible:** Case A — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3079484)
- **"Exceptional cases" reduction (FK 326, part 2):** used to halve the penalty (Case B) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/2882730). Also used to cut a fine to 10% of its amount (2009) — [lex.uz](https://www.lex.uz/uz/docs/2836258)
- **The 50% statutory caps:** Law 670-I art. 25 (0.5% per day, ≤50%) and art. 32 (0.4% per day, ≤50%) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/18942). Plenum 163 p.4 adds that a contractual increase of the statutory penalty is not in itself a ground for reduction — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812)
- **Penalties under void public-procurement contracts:** refused since 2024 (Plenum 163 p.3¹ and p.3²; Plenum 306 p.4) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812); [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546)
- **Acceptance acts:**
  - A unilateral act is valid unless the refusal was justified, and the grounds for justification are narrow (Plenum 306 p.9).
  - An act signed by an unauthorised representative is not acceptance (306 p.11).
  - An unsigned lease transfer act can be overcome by other evidence (234 p.9).
  - For services with a "bilateral act" clause, the case law has gone both ways (Case F).
  — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3106546); [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727); [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/2875644)
- **Force majeure certificates:**
  - The Cabinet of Ministers approved a procedure under which the Chamber of Commerce and Industry (TPP) confirms force majeure for domestic business contracts. Per the search summary, this is Resolution No. 231 of 07.06.2023; I did not confirm the number on the primary text.
  - The TPP received the power in September 2021.
  - A decision is due within 15 days (refusal reasons within 3 days). The certificate is open-ended but covers only the listed contracts.
  - For foreign-trade contracts the competent body is the ministry of investment and foreign trade.
  — [spot.uz](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2023/06/08/force-majeure/)
  - I found no Uzbek court decision on the evidential weight of a TPP certificate.
- **Pretrial claim procedure:**
  - Plenum 234 p.11.1 makes a written warning a pretrial precondition for early lease termination under FK 551 — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2004727)
  - Contracts may make a claim procedure mandatory or not mandatory (Case E) — [lex.uz](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/2839956)
- **Fault defences:** a debtor's lack of funds or a counterparty's breach is not an excuse (Plenum 163 p.12) — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1636812). The contractor in Case B argued that the customer's other contractors caused the delay, and lost — [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/2882730)

### Inferences
- These issues are genuinely debatable because FK 326 gives broad discretion. Its criteria (degree of performance, financial position, creditor's interest, "exceptional cases") are open-textured, and the courts have not published a numeric standard. That makes it ideal for adversarial training:
  - the creditor argues proportionality, the absence of evidence of hardship and the FK 327 floor;
  - the debtor argues partial performance, its financial statements and the absence of creditor losses.
- **Common student mistakes, drawn from the cases:**
  - claiming both penya and FK 327 interest (Case A);
  - claiming both a fine and penya for one breach (Case B);
  - asking for a reduction without financial evidence (Case A);
  - reducing below the FK 327 floor (Case C);
  - ignoring the creditor's own non-performance (Case D);
  - suing a surety after the suretyship term expired (Case E);
  - relying only on the absence of a signed act and ignoring other evidence (Case F, Plenum 234 p.9);
  - suing to "compel signing" of an act or to "declare a contract unconcluded", which are inadmissible claims (Plenum 306 pp.2.2 and 9).

### Gaps
- I did not verify the limitation-period rules (the FK article and term) or any case on them.
- I found no court practice on force majeure certificates.
- I did not find whether the EPC currently makes pretrial claims mandatory generally. The current EPC text should be checked.
